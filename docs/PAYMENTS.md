# Payment settlement and invoice corrections

Clients authorize the full current USD invoice balance before leaving for the builder's own Stripe hosted Checkout. The client pays no surcharge; the builder bears the processor fee. These PAY-001 policy defaults remain ADOPTED pending ct approval.

The office's Billing payment history separates initiated, processing, settled, failed, canceled, ACH returned, disputed, refunded and unresolved instructions. A receipt credits the invoice only after Stripe confirms settlement. Its confirmation source distinguishes a signed event from reconciliation against provider records. Reconcile with Stripe reads the current Checkout and PaymentIntent records, including refunds and disputes. Configure the webhook at `/api/plinth/payments/stripe/events` for Checkout session events, payment intent events, charge events, dispute events and refund events. Signed events trigger the same reconciliation; their arrival order never determines the money balance.

A bank return or card dispute appends a reversal against the original settlement receipt and reopens that amount for collection. A won card dispute restores the previously removed amount. A successful refund removes only the amount Stripe reports refunded; pending refunds do not report money as returned. Original invoice documents and settlement receipts remain intact. The status explanation and receipt history explain each balance change.

If Checkout's response is lost, its durable instruction stays unresolved. The client cannot start another instruction. An owner or administrator selects Reconcile with Stripe: the application searches actual Stripe sessions for that instruction's reference and metadata, then recovers the existing page. Absence from a completed search does not authorize another charge. The application does not automatically resend an unresolved instruction.

Inside each issued invoice, an owner or administrator opens Corrections and refunds. Credit and debit documents retain the original invoice number, terms and reviewed source lines, with their own immutable document numbers, exact tax amounts and balanced accounting entries. Credit tax follows the original and debit tax lots cumulatively so partial credits close every reviewed cent. Debit value cannot exceed unused reviewed selling sources. A void is a full linked credit, permitted only before receipt, allocation or write-off history. A reasoned write-off records a separately disputed unpaid residual as bad debt without receiving funds or reversing sales tax. Clients see public linked documents and balances; private reasons, dispute references and accounting detail remain with the office.

A processor refund requires a linked credit that leaves customer credit. The human reviews its exact amount and reason, then explicitly confirms. The application rereads Stripe settled funds, current human authority and invoice credit before durably reserving the instruction and calling Stripe with its unique idempotency key. Replaying the operation returns the same refund. A lost response is reconciled against Stripe refunds and never sends a second instruction. An unresolved refund reserves its amount; pending and failed outcomes remain distinguishable from returned funds.

Current limits: retained-value invoice corrections and standalone corrections to negative source lines are refused. These remain BILL-005 gaps. The conservative owner and administrator decision authority, explicit audited consent, private reasons and absence of automatic refunds or write-offs remain ADOPTED pending ct approval. BILL-006 inherits processor reconciliation and original-linked reversal history for wider remittance allocation and collection workflows.
