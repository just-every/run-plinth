# Native field time

The native HTTP time workflow is available for manual entry. Installed employee entry, supervisor review and browser location-reminder screens are still pending. This is not a payroll service, a payroll-law determination or proof that a person worked at a location.

All routes below require a current human session and an assigned job for a crew member or superintendent. Writes require a matching Origin, JSON content and the current job `expected_version`. Read the current version from `GET /api/plinth/jobs/:job/time` before writing. Bearer agents, clients, estimators, project managers, bookkeepers and readers have no time door. Custom roles must also hold the corresponding time switch; a copied preset cannot expand its own doors.

- `GET /api/plinth/jobs/:job/time` returns the worker's own entries, correction history, consent choice and latest reminder. Owner and authorized administrator sessions can review all entries and the private rate history. Generic job responses never contain this private custody.
- `POST /api/plinth/jobs/:job/time` enters a completed shift with `dated_on`, accepted `cost_code`, `activity`, elapsed `minutes`, unpaid `break_minutes`, `classification` (`regular` or `overtime`) and an entry `reason`. Workers enter their own time. The office may name an assigned `employee_id`. Split a crew day into entries on the actual jobs and codes. Reasons and activities are shared with the employee.
- `POST /api/plinth/jobs/:job/time/:entry/correct` appends a full replacement of those entry fields and a reason. Earlier revisions remain. Approved entries cannot be edited; existing recorded cost corrections remain separate and do not rewrite their timesheet or payroll export.
- `POST /api/plinth/jobs/:job/time/rates` is owner-only. Supply an assigned `employee_id`, integer `hour_cents`, `burden_bps`, `overtime_bps` (10000 means the ordinary rate), `human_reviewed: true` and a source `reference`. Rates append to history. The latest reviewed job rate at approval is frozen into the approved source; later changes do not reprice it.
- `POST /api/plinth/jobs/:job/time/:entry/approve` is for the owner or an authorized administrator with financial visibility. The employee and anyone who entered or corrected that time cannot approve it. The last revision posts to the actual job and code once, within the open accounting period. Paid minutes equal elapsed minutes less unpaid breaks. An overtime hourly rate is rounded half-up to cents first; gross is rounded half-up from paid minutes times that rate divided by 60; burden is rounded half-up from gross times its basis-point rate divided by 10000. No unapproved entry posts costs.
- `GET /api/plinth/jobs/:job/time/payroll.csv` is an audited office-only allocation export containing approved entries, employee and job ids, code, date, classification, paid minutes, gross, burden and the linked time source. Each job has its own export. It does not send payroll or claim a Gusto connection. The existing COST-003 payroll reconciliation can use the linked source to replace provisional cost once with externally recorded actual gross and burden.

## Optional site reminders

The following policy was approved by ct on 24 October 2026 at 21:12Z. Only the owner configures a site using `POST /api/plinth/jobs/:job/time/site` with a visible `label`, site `latitude`, `longitude` and `radius_meters`. The owner can disable it with `enabled: false`.

The worker explicitly opts in with `POST /api/plinth/jobs/:job/time/consent` and `consent: true`, or revokes with `consent: false`. Consent changes are audited. Revocation clears the latest inside status and reminder. A site configuration change also clears those states.

`POST /api/plinth/jobs/:job/time/location` accepts the worker's current `latitude` and `longitude` only after their consent gate passes. It calculates whether the point is inside the configured radius, including its boundary. The first inside event produces an arrival reminder; a later crossing produces the corresponding arrival or departure reminder. Repeated events on the same side do not produce another reminder. A first outside event establishes state without claiming a departure. The response states that coordinates are discarded and no time was recorded. Worker coordinates are not stored. Only consent, the latest inside flag and the latest reminder remain in private job custody. No background location collection, automatic clock action or outbound message exists.

Private installation archives carry the job's time records and site policy under the existing job custody and legal-hold rules. Erasing the job removes that custody. Future FIELD-003, INT-010, NOTIFY-001 and AUTO-003 doors inherit the same current permissions, consent, immutable source links and human approval boundary before shipping.
