# Job cost ledger

The native cost door is `/api/plinth/jobs/<job-id>/costs`. Writes require a human session, a matching Origin and the current `expected_version`. Owners and administrators retain office access. Assigned bookkeepers enter and code bills and reconcile; designated managers approve separately. Readers receive only the granted draw packet. Custom roles use registered feature permissions and financial visibility. Generic job responses omit cost originals and accountant authority. Financial review uses the existing app components for bill entry, recoding, separate manager posting, lender evidence and accountant review.

`GET costs` returns USD rows and totals; `GET costs?code=06-100` selects one code. Every row retains source ids, dates, accounting periods, original/revised budget revisions, PO/receipt sources, actual entries and client billing/collection sources. Unknown estimate costs stay `null`. Legacy uncoded amounts remain `Unassigned`; their costs are never silently distributed. Approved SOV applications supply billed value; their invoices do not bill it a second time. Direct invoice receipts, reversals and applied deposits supply collected value, with unapplied deposit money kept Unassigned. This is a current ledger, not a closed-period report. An accounting period names the posting period; it does not claim accountant approval or period closure (COST-005).

Open commitment is issued commitment less matched bill subtotal, net of credits. Received-not-billed is net receipt value less that consumption. Actual is posted bill total including reviewed tax, net of credit totals. Actual plus open commitment therefore counts received goods once. Separately unmatched bill tax is actual cost, rather than another receipt consumption. Original budget is the first accepted cost snapshot; revised estimate is the latest approved revision; approved budget includes approved bid cost adjustments. Uncommitted forecast initially means the nonnegative residual of a known approved cost budget after actual and open commitment, and remains unknown when that budget is unknown. Quantified forecasts will replace that residual under COST-004. Source histories remain fixed.

## Supplier bills

`POST costs/bills` captures `vendor`, `reference`, `dated_on`, `due_on`, `period` (`YYYY-MM`), `document`, `tax`, and `lines`. A document has a filename, PDF/PNG/JPEG/CSV MIME type and base64 original bytes (maximum 1 MB). Original bytes and SHA-256 stay in the private job archive. `GET costs/bills/<id>/original` returns the identical attachment to the office only. `GET costs/bills` returns metadata without original bytes.

Each line has `cost_code`, `description`, positive integer `subtotal_cents`, explicit `taxable`, and receipt `matches`. A match names `order_id`, `receipt_id`, the receipt's `cost_code` and positive `amount_cents`. Matches may split an invoice across deliveries and codes. Their sum cannot exceed the line subtotal or the remaining receipt value. The invoice vendor must equal the PO supplier. Missing receipts and different receipt/actual codes require a human review. Subcontract PO matches also require released scope, recorded progress within its draw amount, and resolved waiver gates. Subcontract progress and separate retained/payable balances are exercised through the same native matching door.

Tax takes `percent`, `reference`, `exemption_reference` for any exempt line, and `human_reviewed: true`. The existing EST-003 exact pricing engine calculates tax once, half up, and distributes any rounding remainder in source order. No markup or discount is added to a supplier's stated subtotal. The retained tax snapshot says human reviewed, never provider verified. Posting cannot rewrite that snapshot.

`POST costs/bills/<id>/review` retains a reason and the human reviewer. `POST costs/bills/<id>/recode` takes `line_id`, `cost_code`, and `reason`, resets review, and records before/after coding. `POST costs/bills/<id>/post` validates current receipt balances again, posts exactly once, records approval and retains the original. A repeated vendor/reference is refused across the installation, including other jobs. Posted records cannot be recoded; use a linked credit or an explicit correction.

A credit is a new original with `kind: credit`, `credit_of` the posted bill id, its own reference and positive line values. Posting writes negative actual/payable and consumption entries; the original stays unchanged. Credits cannot exceed the original bill/code/matched receipt. Payable means recorded bill liability net of credits, separate supplier retainage and matched recorded supplier draws. Staff-recorded supplier payments cite their original reference and do not post another actual. Unmatched paid draw value remains an explicit cost exception; PAY-003 owns later net payment handoff. Plinth neither moves nor independently verifies funds.

“Post” means the native ledger only. QBO/Xero sync remains held under INT-001, INT-002 and INT-003, requiring authorized provider company grants. No request was sent to a provider. The job-scoped cost records and views below inherit RPT-004 typed fields and native stage requirements.

## Routed approval and financial fields

A bill names `reviewer_id`, an active native owner or administrator. It defaults to the configured exception owner, or the preparing office person until an owner defines policy. `POST costs/bills/<id>/route` changes that reviewer with a reason. Only the designated current office reviewer posts. Separation is on when no policy exists: the preparer cannot approve their own bill or cost source. An owner can explicitly configure a different separation rule; changing it is audited. Over-limit bills require the owner as reviewer. A subcontract match also supplies the exact `draw_id`; matching cannot exceed that released draw or bypass its accepted waivers or already recorded owner exception. Gross actual cost remains separate from supplier retained liability. A later supplier retainage release changes payable and retained balances without posting another actual cost or touching client retainage.

The existing financial custom-field schema applies to bills, time/expense/equipment/payroll sources and forecasts. `custom_fields` on a native mutation merges validated values and checks the real destination stage (`draft`, `posted`, `approved`, or `recorded`). `GET costs/records` discovers these record kinds, their typed columns and values within this job. `POST costs/values/<local-id>` edits fields or a named metadata stage; it cannot post a bill or approve time. `POST costs/query` and `POST costs/views` use the existing RPT-004 view shape (financial scope, explicit record IDs or null for all supplied job records, filters and selected columns). `GET costs/views/<id>` runs a saved view; `/export.csv` exports the same annotated scope and safe spreadsheet cells. Personal views remain creator-only, shared views still require current native office authority on every request. The supplied universe is this job's cost records, explicitly labelled rather than a whole portfolio. Existing generic Reports continues to discover the older financial kinds; this scoped cost door supplies the new ones.

## Time, payroll, equipment and expenses

`POST costs/sources` prepares a `time`, `expense` or `equipment` record with a unique source `reference`, `dated_on`, accounting `period` and accepted `cost_code`. Time names `employee_reference`, positive integer `minutes`, integer `hour_cents` and optional integer `burden_bps`. Equipment names `equipment_reference`, minutes and hourly cents. Base cost is minutes × hourly cents / 60, rounded half up, then burden is base × basis points / 10,000, rounded half up. Both rates and calculations stay in the original. An expense names an employee reference, positive integer `amount_cents` and its original `document`. `POST costs/sources/<id>/approve` posts once; `.../reimburse` records explicit external reimbursement evidence with `confirm: true` and never posts another actual. `GET .../original` downloads the identical private employee receipt and records its access.

`POST costs/payroll` accepts `csv`, a reviewed register `reference`, date and period. This is a documented native allocation CSV, not a claim of compatibility with a provider export:

```csv
external_id,time_source_id,gross_cents,burden_cents
synthetic-plinth-payroll-1,approved-timesheet-id,25000,5500
```

Fields are unquoted ASCII identifiers and nonnegative integer cents; one to 100 rows are accepted atomically, with each payroll identity and timesheet once. Gross plus actual burden replaces the approved timesheet's provisional amount by a linked difference entry. Original time, its approved rate and the entire exact CSV remain retained. A repeat import is refused. A transferred timesheet must be reconciled before payroll import; payroll cannot silently post on its old job. `GET costs/payroll/<import-id>/original` retrieves exact CSV bytes with an access event. This is ledger allocation only: Gusto connection, payroll execution, tax filing and money movement remain held under INT-010 and BOUND-001.

`POST costs/transfers` takes an original positive `entry_id`, `target_job_id`, `target_expected_version`, `target_cost_code`, positive cents, date, period, unique correction reference and reason. Opposite entries and human history commit in both jobs in one D1 transaction; both versions must be current. The original remains, and the source cannot be transferred beyond its remaining allocation. Corrections move actual cost, never a PO, receipt, commitment or funds.

Existing CARE-003 callback costs already contribute actual cost by source id. An employee expense may name its exact `warranty_cost_id`; amount and code must agree and it can link once. Approving or reimbursing that expense retains its receipt without posting the callback a second time.

## Forecasts and source exceptions

`POST costs/forecasts` records immutable `as_of`, `source_as_of` freshness evidence and one assumption for every code in the current ledger. Each code supplies `remaining_quantity`, `minutes_per_unit`, `labor_hour_cents`, `outstanding_commitment_cents`, `unbought_cents`, `risk_cents` and a source `reference`. Quantities/productivity accept nonnegative decimals with up to three places. Unbought labor is quantity × minutes per unit × hourly cents / 60, rounded half up once; remaining cost then adds exactly the current unconsumed commitment, unbought nonlabor and risk. Final cost is actual plus remaining; projected gross margin is approved code revenue minus final cost; variance is final cost minus known approved budget. A `pending` scenario may include a signed `pending_change_cents` assumption without treating its revenue as client-approved. Assumptions and source digest remain with every snapshot; a later job version marks the older forecast stale. `GET costs/forecasts` compares retained snapshots. The existing application download and History approved records retain their code ledger and assumption evidence.

`POST costs/policy` is owner-only and requires explicit `budget_limit_cents`, `commitment_limit_cents`, `bill_limit_cents`, `separate_approval`, an active office `owner_id` for follow-up and `due_on`. Budget exposure is posted actual plus unconsumed commitment, so independent actual and commitment subtotals cannot hide a combined overrun. Overruns, unmatched receipts, unposted bills, unapproved time/expenses and missing or stale accounting reconciliation are visible; configured policy assigns exceptions with sources and due dates. `POST costs/reconcile` records a reviewed accounting `period`, explicit code/amount `balances` (an empty ledger is explicit) and source `reference`. It neither removes native postings nor claims a provider sync. Source changes invalidate its fingerprint. `GET costs/exceptions` distinguishes open, awaiting human review and cleared history. `POST costs/exceptions/<id>` requires resolution evidence and refuses clearance while the underlying source amounts still disagree. Corrected or posted sources do not silently clear their earlier follow-ups. A new recurrence gets a new retained exception.

COST-006 uses its stated owner-defined limits and approval separation: only the owner configures them; only active native owners/administrators handle current cost data; the designated reviewer posts bills; separation defaults on until explicit audited owner configuration. There is no new external/custom-role access, outbound or destructive accounting action. ROLE-004 and ROLE-006 inherit these checks before extending accountant or custom-role access.

## Cash, WIP and periods (native partial)

`GET costs/cash?start_period=2026-10&months=3&opening_cash_cents=20000` projects one to 24 months from a stated opening amount. Sources are posted bills net of their linked credits, held supplier retainage and matched staff-recorded supplier draw payments, remaining issued-PO delivery commitments after bill consumption, approved time replaced by payroll actuals, unreimbursed expenses, issued deposit/draw invoice balances, separately retained supplier/client retainage, and the latest forecast's unbought labor/material/risk. Equipment operating allocations are costs, not invented cash payments. Sources without an expected date remain explicitly outside the horizon; absent quantity forecasts are labelled incomplete. Dates and retained values are projections, never bank balances, payment execution, accountant certification or promised funds.

`POST costs/cash-dates` retains `kind` (`bill`, `invoice`, `source`, `order`, `retention` or `forecast`), an existing `source_id`, `expected_on` and reviewed evidence `reference`. Later dates append rather than replace history. Forecast source IDs are `<forecast-id>:<cost-code>`; retention IDs are a supplier draw or issued client invoice. An expected retainage date never releases it; BILL-003's human evidence gates still apply.

The WIP tie-out uses latest retained forecast final cost, or the known approved budget residual where no quantity forecast exists. Unknown costs leave WIP unavailable. Cost-based earned value is approved gross contract cents × actual / final cost, capped from zero to full contract and rounded half up once; under/over-billing compares that value with native client billed value. It includes the contract's reviewed tax in the same gross basis as the frozen budgets and is an explicit projection for review, not a claim of accounting-standard certification. Forecast source freshness is shown.

The owner designates an existing job-assigned bookkeeper account at `POST /api/plinth/jobs/<job-id>/costs/accountant`, with a reason; a null accountant id revokes the designation. The signed-in accountant requests `GET /api/plinth/jobs/<job-id>/costs/period-preview`, reviews the returned exact snapshot and confirms its digest, evidence and current job version at `POST /api/plinth/jobs/<job-id>/costs/period-reviews`. Only the owner records the close at `POST /api/plinth/jobs/<job-id>/costs/periods`, supplying the native review id and identical month, opening cash and horizon. Current account status, job assignment, designation and review permissions are checked again. A changed job requires a fresh review. The retained close copies the exact reviewed snapshot. An external accountant fact remains explicitly recorded by owner and never claims authenticated accountant approval. Closed snapshots cannot be replaced.

Late bill or approved time/expense/equipment costs retain their original date and choose an explicit open `posting_period`; payroll allocations and cross-job transfers name an open `period`. Late native client receipts, supplier receipts and callback costs additionally need `posting_period` and `adjustment_reference` when their source date falls in a closed month. Their original date remains, their accounting period is explicit, and closed snapshots stay fixed. Native bill posting does not move funds; PAY-003 still owns supplier payment handoff.


Native accountant review records the signed-in account and time, without professional certification. The close is an owner-recorded fact. No automatic close or funds movement occurs. Future accounting connectors inherit explicit ledger authority under ROLE-004.
