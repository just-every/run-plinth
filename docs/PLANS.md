# Versioned plans

Open a job’s **Plans** tab to upload original PDF plans, specifications and addenda. Each sheet records its number, revision, PDF page and issue date. A plan set may contain multiple originals. A repeated sheet number uses a new revision number. Uploading a draft never replaces the approved field drawing.

An owner, administrator or assigned project manager reviews the exact set and chooses currently authorized recipients before **Approve and notify recipients**. Approval puts a durable notice in each recipient’s **Plan inbox**. It does not send email or claim provider delivery. Opening a notice does not acknowledge it: the addressed person acknowledges that exact set separately. Repeating approval or acknowledgment preserves the original distribution and attribution.

Assigned field staff open the latest approved sheet by default. Every sheet has a pinned link identifying its exact source set and sheet. Older sources retain a prominent superseded label, original PDF bytes and their own annotations. Annotations never move to a newer revision. Plan sets, original bytes, annotations and acknowledgments travel with the existing native job archive; restored authentication and external authority remain subject to the receiving installation’s existing rules.

The plan doors inherit current ROLE-001 and ROLE-002 assignments, ROLE-003 client and released-company contacts, ROLE-006 document switches, and NATIVE-009 optimistic write and current-authority fences. Recipients are selected only from active people with current job authority. External plan access additionally requires a distribution to that person and the job’s enabled Plans portal feature. A notice creates no job grant. Clients and trades see only their own acknowledgments. The native archive and job erasure duties remain with NATIVE-011, NATIVE-012 and NATIVE-013; DOC-007 retains general folders and selective miscellaneous-file sharing, and NOTIFY-001 retains outbound delivery channels.
