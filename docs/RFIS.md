# RFIs and design responses

Open a job’s **RFIs** tab. Owners and administrators grant personally invited field, designer and trade contacts access to the job’s separate RFI inbox. Choose which source drawing sheets a field contact may reference. A trade contact must be an assigned contact of its own released company scope; the grant never exposes another company’s rates, W-9 or contacts. Removing that company contact, revoking the RFI grant or disabling the account removes access on the next request, including original-file downloads.

Field staff and subs raise a question with an exact sheet revision/page, optional cost code, affected tasks, assigned designer, due date and up to five original PDFs, PNGs or JPEGs (2 MB each). Existing takeoff sheets provide the current native drawing door; DOC-001 and DOC-007 inherit broader plan distribution and selective sharing when they ship. Old source links retain the exact original bytes.

The assigned designer or an owner/administrator records a response with revised detail originals, proposed total client price impact and whole calendar-day impact. This is a recorded human design answer, not Plinth certification of design or licensure. Response revisions append without replacing earlier answers.

An owner/administrator routes the RFI to affected granted contacts and publishes the exact response, explicitly reviewing its price/date impacts and recipients. Publication distributes the reviewed answer through those contacts’ native RFI inboxes; no email send is claimed. Draft answers remain visible only to the office and their author. Affected trades see the design answer and pending-change state, without proposed client pricing or other recipients’ acknowledgments. Each recipient explicitly acknowledges the exact response revision. Reading it never acknowledges it.

A response with a price/date impact prepares one draft native client change. Its economic delta is the proposed total price impact less the last approved RFI price impact, in integer USD cents. Revised responses invalidate older pending changes while keeping all approved decisions. A date-only impact can prepare a zero-price change. Affected tasks and successors remain held from further schedule publication until the assigned client approves the linked change. Neither the response nor client approval sends an order, publishes revised dates or certifies physical work. A builder must separately review and publish any dates. CLIENT-004 inherits electronic execution and full date revisions; SCH-004/NOTIFY-001 inherit outbound notification delivery.

Resolve after the current response’s affected recipients acknowledged it and its client change is approved. Resolution and reopening retain the actor, time and reason. Owner archives preserve the questions, response revisions, grants and checksummed originals; sessions and credentials remain excluded.

The conservative RFI grant/distribution policy awaits ct approval, so DOC-003 remains partial. For a local walkthrough, `rer dev --as field`, `rer dev --as designer` and `rer dev --as trade` create ordinary local member personas. They have no RFI or job authority until the owner grants it through the same native doors.
