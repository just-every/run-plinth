# Plinth's company agent door

The Construction project coordinator uses one shared Base door. Discover modern and 2025-11-25 MCP at `/mcp`, canonical resource metadata at `/.well-known/oauth-protected-resource/mcp`, signed company policy at `/api/rer/agent-policy`, and the native person controls at `/agents`. The committed `agent-surface.json` is the machine-readable registry and disposable installation journey.

Supported tools inspect currently visible job identities and job records, discover native catalog and reviewed tax-rate identities, prepare estimates, draft bookings, private daily logs, changes, supplier orders and reconciled pay apps, retain and revise original takeoff drawings, calibrate and measure them, capture and refresh supplied product evidence, add already human-reviewed product sources to estimates, and preview catalog, tax and measured pricing. The declared CORE subset exposes tools, policy, pause, audit and idempotency. Events, public resource reads, prompts and subscriptions are undeclared and return typed refusals. Internal native resource adapters retain visibility checks for audit; resource links are withheld. Source and imported text is untrusted; it grants no authority. Missing and inaccessible resources have the same response.

Business `owner_id` binds to the claimed native owner. Signed teammate IDs bind to native operator IDs, preserving that identity through email changes. Business administrators are policy editors; Business members have no policy role. A native promotion alone does not make someone a Business editor. Current account admission, local role and custom permission switches, and job assignments apply on every call. Custom Administrators retain the native Administrator job visibility; assigned project roles retain their job restrictions when acting through an agent. A native mutation checks the current person/version, job version, team/custom-role versions, agent revocation and policy/settings fence in the same D1 transaction as its draft and native activity.

Draft tools compute standard or sensitive classes from proposed content and relevant native source records; calibration and identity catalogues remain standard. They ignore supplied risk and confirmation labels. They save the native target, proposed items, amount and supplier where applicable for policy review. Current source revisions bind held work. Native record history and activity preserve both agent and sponsor. Retries with one key do not create another effect; changed arguments conflict. There is no registered provider cost or spending-cap enforcement, and no invented Undo for append-only originals or drafts without a native reversal.

The current NATIVE-008 contract remains draft-only. Policy never permits an agent to approve a quote, make a client decision, publish a diary or schedule, issue or receive an order, send correspondence, create a charge, request payment/refund, change standing access, import, export or erase. Those actions stay at their existing named-person doors. Product review, final estimate pricing and takeoff adoption also require their existing native person decisions. Device, screen-based extraction, connector, import and installation doors retain their purposes. The wider autonomous construction week described by the separate draft Agent surface is not a shipped capability.

The native setup-token owner claim remains the installation and verification bootstrap. The fixture drives `/auth/bootstrap` and ordinary native HTTP actions with its returned owner session; it creates no people or job rows through SQL. The adopted tools do not call Stripe, webhooks, OAuth connectors, LLMs or storage providers. Product URLs are retained evidence, not fetched. No extra verification network edge is declared; native invitation mail uses the shared built-in mail adapters.

Runtime source is copied from Base `114ef7a61f080da053af783c5fbe06f625d692cc` (rer-v0.2.88); the released npm CLI is pinned separately to `0.2.88`. Normal `pnpm exec rer verify` uses that installed CLI. The Base source, module source digests and additive migrations travel with the application; no generated CLI copy is edited into this seed. Existing migrations retain their applied bytes.

The subset does not claim multi-sponsor, timed or queued lifecycles, resource refresh, dual-host work or owner-native administration. The wider draft SURFACE-001 through SURFACE-013 construction week remains partial: final actions are natively person-only, spend caps are unenforced, and this journey proves the supported draft registry rather than those target-only jobs.
