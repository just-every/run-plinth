# Plinth's company agent door

The Construction project coordinator uses one shared Base door. Discover modern and 2025-11-25 MCP at `/mcp`, canonical resource metadata at `/.well-known/oauth-protected-resource/mcp`, signed company policy at `/api/rer/agent-policy`, and the native person controls at `/agents`. The committed `agent-surface.json` is the machine-readable registry and disposable installation journey.

Supported tools inspect currently visible job identities and job records, discover native catalog and reviewed tax-rate identities, prepare estimates, draft bookings, private daily logs, changes, supplier orders and reconciled pay apps, retain and revise original takeoff drawings, calibrate and measure them, capture and refresh supplied product evidence, add already human-reviewed product sources to estimates, and preview catalog, tax and measured pricing. `plinth.events.list` exposes retained events. Fixed company records, catalogs, tax rates, vendor summaries and native job projections are resources. Source and imported text is untrusted; it grants no authority. Missing and inaccessible resources have the same response.

Business `owner_id` binds to the claimed native owner. Signed teammate IDs bind to native operator IDs, preserving that identity through email changes. Business administrators are policy editors; Business members have no policy role. A native promotion alone does not make someone a Business editor. Current account admission, local role and custom permission switches, and job assignments apply on every call. Custom Administrators retain the native Administrator job visibility; assigned project roles retain their job restrictions when acting through an agent. A native mutation checks the current person/version, job version, team/custom-role versions, agent revocation and policy/settings fence in the same D1 transaction as its draft and native activity.

Draft tools compute standard or sensitive classes from proposed content and relevant native source records; calibration and identity catalogues remain standard. They ignore supplied risk and confirmation labels. They save the native target, proposed items, amount and supplier where applicable for policy review. Current source revisions bind held work. Native record history and activity preserve both agent and sponsor. Retries with one key do not create another effect; changed arguments conflict. There is no registered provider cost or spending-cap enforcement, and no invented Undo for append-only originals or drafts without a native reversal.

The current NATIVE-008 contract remains draft-only. Policy never permits an agent to approve a quote, make a client decision, publish a diary or schedule, issue or receive an order, send correspondence, create a charge, request payment/refund, change standing access, import, export or erase. Those actions stay at their existing named-person doors. Product review, final estimate pricing and takeoff adoption also require their existing native person decisions. Device, screen-based extraction, connector, import and installation doors retain their purposes. The wider autonomous construction week described by the separate draft Agent surface is not a shipped capability.

The native setup-token owner claim remains the installation and verification bootstrap. The fixture drives `/auth/bootstrap` and ordinary native HTTP actions with its returned owner session; it creates no people or job rows through SQL. The adopted tools do not call Stripe, webhooks, OAuth connectors, LLMs or storage providers. Product URLs are retained evidence, not fetched. No extra verification network edge is declared; native invitation mail uses the shared built-in mail adapters.

## Private closeout evidence

The signed-in owner can read the job’s exact closeout source inventory at `/api/plinth/jobs/{job_id}/agent-closeout`, then POST a title, `source_keys`, explicitly recorded `requirements` and the current `expected_version` to that route. Each requirement names its evidence kind, exact selected source keys (empty when missing), a proposed owner and the source or reason for the requirement. The packet records original identities, revisions, checksums and native download paths alongside unresolved punch and service evidence. Recorded document evidence is never a certification or a completed repair.

POST to `/api/plinth/jobs/{job_id}/agent-closeout/{packet_id}/review` with its `digest`, `confirm: true`, review statement and current job version to consent to bounded agent use of that exact private packet. `plinth.closeout.read` accepts a job and optional packet identity; omitting the identity reads the latest reviewed packet. `plinth.closeout.followup` accepts an exact packet identity and digest, a draft request identity, request or visit kind, requirement identities, proposed owner, unsent message and a proposed window for a visit. It prepares an append-only unsent draft. Identical retries return the same draft; changing terms under the same request identity is refused. Policy approval concerns drafting only.

The signed-in owner can revoke packet consent at `/api/plinth/jobs/{job_id}/agent-closeout/{packet_id}/revoke` with the exact digest, current job version, confirmation and a reason; history stays private and further agent use is refused. Source changes invalidate reviewed packets and cached or held reads. Prepare and review a successor; earlier packets and draft history remain intact. The general job projection never includes this private packet library. Current owner sponsorship, packet consent and job authority are checked at the packet door; restored consent stays inactive until a fresh owner-reviewed packet is prepared. Erasing a job also removes its closeout replay content and saved approval material. Adding a manual or receiving a trade statement cannot certify an inspection, interpret coverage, book a visit, release work, send a request or close a service case. Those decisions remain at their existing human doors.
