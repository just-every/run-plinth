# Private collection balance reviews

The signed-in owner opens a job's Billing screen, reviews current sources and saves a collection review. The review binds exact issued invoice originals by digest and freezes their monetary terms, receipts and original-linked returns, deposit allocations, adjustments, write-offs, remittances, retainage and follow-up rule revisions. Repeated identical reviews under current owner authority retain one identity without another write. Source changes need a new review; prior reviews remain readable as history. Revocation appends a reason without rewriting the original review.

The current collectible invoice residual excludes held retainage. Unapplied remittance and unapplied deposit liability are separate; an unallocated amount does not pay an invoice automatically. Deposit requests due remain separate from earned invoice residuals. An invoice's exact current balance may be negative after a credit; the collectible residual is then zero, while the signed balance and all adjustment sources remain visible.

Dated previews reuse the current collection sequence's recipients, consent, pause, quiet hours, review limits, mailbox suppression and grants. Each invoice shows its current residual and whether its monetary leg is outstanding, covered by recorded receipts, reopened after a native bank or check return, reduced by a write-off or adjustment, or unresolved at the processor boundary. Changed balances produce changed prospective action identities. No sequence is dispatched or enabled by a review.

Recorded bank and check evidence is staff-recorded, never independently verified funds. Full recorded coverage stops only that monetary reminder leg. Zero recorded due, write-offs, prepared messages and previews never close a collection case or establish receipt delivery. Processor events, disputes, receipt classification, delivery and redelivery, failed-delivery recovery and final independently reconciled closure remain with PAY-001, PAY-002, BILL-006, INT-007 and NOTIFY-001. Existing collection ownership, approval and provider-grant boundaries remain unchanged.

Only the dedicated owner door returns private review content. Generic job responses, nonowners and bearer agents receive no review payload. Every read or replay reloads the job and current authority. Erasure removes the review with its job and fences captured requests. Archives preserve exact review history and sources, but the receiving installation's changed authority generation prevents old preview use or replayed consent. A newly fetched source and authority context plus explicit owner review creates a new review there; restoration never authorizes sending or financial action.

Native HTTP doors are rooted at `/api/plinth/jobs/{job_id}/collection-reviews`: GET lists current balances and retained history; POST explicitly confirms the returned source digest, job version and authority context. GET on a review identity reads its immutable history; GET on its `preview` child requires current source and authority and a dated `as_of` instant. POST on its `revoke` child records a reason and explicit confirmation. No door posts a receipt or return, approves money, assigns collections, sends a message or contacts a provider.
