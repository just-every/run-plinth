# Warranty service records

The native HTTP service workflow builds on the component terms and reminder agreements in **Warranty**. Dedicated client and trade screens are not yet implemented. The service sharing policy below is conservative and was ratified by ct with changes on 2026-10-23 (see CARE-002); CARE-002 remains partial.

An assigned job client reads `GET /api/plinth/jobs/:job/service` and submits a request to the same path with the current `expected_version`, a listed `component_id`, `description`, `availability`, and one to five original PNG/JPEG `attachments` in the standard `{name, mime, base64}` shape, up to 2 MB each. Submission records a request only: it sends no message, determines no coverage and books no work.

A current owner, administrator or assigned project manager acknowledges ownership with `POST .../service/:case/acknowledge-owner` and a reason. That person becomes the designated coverage reviewer under the existing `warranties/:case/review` door; a reasoned coverage decision remains a human judgment under the signed terms.

The office uses `.../service/:case/assign` with `subcontract_id`, `email` and a reason to route the case to an active invited contact of that company's own released scope. Assignment grants only the case, request photos and availability, appointments, shared coverage decisions and repair history. It gives no whole-job, client, office-cost, private coverage-source or other-company access. External views and action responses omit award identifiers, directory emails and internal attribution IDs; attributions contain display name, role and time only. After reassignment, the new company receives only its own full visits, evidence, reviews and originals; an earlier company's visit shows only its dates and the builder's outcome and reason, and the earlier company loses case access. Removing a contact, holding its award, disabling its account or the warranty portal feature ends inbox and file access on the next request.

`.../service/:case/book` takes explicit timezone-qualified `start` and `end`, an optional whole-minute `travel_minutes` and a reason. It books a real task through ordinary cross-job conflict and resource checks; it does not send an invitation or claim attendance. For jobs using the reviewed planner, publish the appointment through that planner and supply its exact booked `task_id` instead. Linking checks the current trade, requires a booked non-milestone task, prevents reuse and never rewrites the reviewed schedule. Current appointment reads reflect moves/cancellations of the linked task, while each visit retains its original agreed interval.

The trade reads `GET /api/plinth/warranty-service`, downloads only its assigned cases' originals at `/api/plinth/warranty-service/:job/files/:file`, and posts its exact `visit_id`, reason and original evidence to `/api/plinth/warranty-service/:job/:case/submit`. The office accepts or rejects that exact submission at the job service `.../:case/review` door with `visit_id`, boolean `accepted` and a reason. A rejection stays open and allows a separately booked return visit; it preserves the first evidence and review.

An accepted fix stays open until an assigned client explicitly posts `visit_id` and reason to `.../:case/acknowledge-client`. The latest visit must have builder acceptance and a resolved coverage decision. The atomic closure retains both decisions, every visit and all evidence. Office callers cannot bypass client acknowledgment through the older warranty-close door. Closure pauses explicitly agreed case reminders under CARE-003, without altering signed component terms or erasing history. Rejected fixes and builder acceptance alone never stop those reminders. No new automatic outbound action is introduced. The separate no-repair closure workflow below implements the native HTTP part of CARE-005; dedicated client and builder screens remain unbuilt.

Every mutation requires the current job `expected_version`, a signed-in person and matching Origin. Bearer agents are refused. Owners may disable `warranty` independently in the client and subcontractor portal presets. Private archives already carry job records and original attachment bytes; no new session or credential is included.

## Closing without an accepted repair (CARE-005, partial)

A currently assigned signed-in client posts a reason to `.../service/:case/withdraw-client` to retain an explicit withdrawal statement. The case and its reminders stay open until an owner, administrator or assigned project manager posts a reasoned human builder resolution to `.../service/:case/resolve-no-repair`. That resolution closes the withdrawn request even if there has been no visit or coverage review.

Without withdrawal, the designated reviewer's latest coverage decision must be `not_covered`. The builder posts that exact `review_id` and reason to `.../resolve-no-repair`; this records a resolution but leaves the case and reminders open. An assigned client then posts the exact `review_id`, `resolution_id` and explicit acknowledgment reason to `.../acknowledge-not-covered`. A newer coverage review or builder resolution fences a stale acknowledgment. `needs_evidence` and a covered request without accepted repair cannot use this not-covered closure path. A client may explicitly withdraw a covered request instead.

Office-only, agent and trade closure or client-statement impersonation are refused. Closure is version-fenced, retains photos, visits, decisions, statements and originals, leaves closed history immutable, and pauses only that case's active agreed reminders without changing component terms or coverage. It sends no notice. Every action requires the current `expected_version` and a matching-origin native session.

Ownership, assignment, booking, repair/review, resolution and acknowledgment reasons entered through these HTTP doors are shared with the assigned clients and current company. Do not enter office-private notes in them; private coverage sources remain in the office CARE-003 records. Dedicated entry-screen sharing labels and the full 1440/390 service journeys remain unbuilt under CARE-002/CARE-005.
