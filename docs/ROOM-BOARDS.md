# Room boards

The owner can open **Room boards** inside a job to collect room measurements, design originals and finish annotations. Each save creates a new immutable revision. A board is private to the current installation owner; neither a client assignment nor a link to a selection grants board access.

Upload PDF, PNG, JPEG, GLB, OBJ or IFC originals, up to 2 MB each. The built-in PDF viewer opens individual pages. Images open beside revision-specific finish markers. Each original has a download that returns its exact stored bytes, including after later revisions. Additional originals join the next revision; earlier revisions remain available in the revision selector.

GLB version 2 models must contain an embedded buffer and a static scene with triangle primitives. The viewer applies node transforms. OBJ positions and polygon faces become static triangle geometry. Both views support rotation, tilt and zoom, with at most 12000 vertices and 12000 triangles. Materials, textures and animations are not rendered. Skins, morph targets, required extensions, external buffers and unsupported primitives produce a visible unsupported-model result. No model resources are fetched from another host. Unsupported originals remain available to download. IFC2X3 and IFC4 STEP originals can display tessellated triangle bodies, convex planar faceted boundary representations and rectangular extrusions. SI metre prefixes, nested local placements and representation contexts are applied, and IFC Z-up coordinates are converted to the viewer's Y-up metres. Unsupported body types, holes, units and transforms refuse the whole model rather than displaying a misleading partial building. IFC styling, material properties and non-body representations are explicitly reported as unrendered. Complete model-format coverage remains unfinished.

Link a reviewed supplier capture and an exact existing selection revision to compare the chosen specification, cost code, supplier cost and client price with the room plan. The board retains those snapshots when supplier prices or selections later change. Updating a board never signs a selection, changes an accepted estimate, revises construction scope or replaces a signed specification. Those remain separate reviewed workflows.

**Export this room revision** downloads a private ZIP with `board.json`, the exact measurements, annotations, product and signed-selection snapshots, original checksums and every original byte. Original filenames are recorded in the manifest. Each supported model also has neutral OBJ geometry. GLB and IFC sources produce flattened GLB and IFC4 triangle artifacts in metres; their source transforms are retained in the coordinates, and IFC exports use Z-up. Uncalibrated OBJ originals have unspecified units, so their neutral export stays OBJ. Neutral geometry does not claim original materials, animation, building semantics or professional certification. Unsupported originals remain in the package with their refusal reason. Exporting an earlier revision after later edits still returns the earlier sources and signed specification. A download confers no external access or delivery authority.

Under **Link an authorized walkthrough**, the owner can record an HTTPS design-tool tour or render URL and its title after explicitly confirming authority to use it. Links belong to immutable board revisions, can be removed in a new revision and are never fetched or embedded automatically. Choosing a link opens a separate window without a referrer; the design tool must independently authorize access. Storing a URL is not a receipt proving provider permissions, a working tour or an external grant.

The revision link opens that exact board revision on another device after owner sign-in. It is not an external sharing grant or a complete walkthrough service. Client and subcontractor presentations, external distribution, complete walkthrough handoff and owner-authorized provider round trips remain unfinished. The external link journey uses a network boundary fixture and establishes navigation only. Live Autodesk access still needs an owner-authorized project and application grant under INT-012; no such grant is supplied to this run. Room data and originals travel in the private installation archive and obey the job's hold and erasure fences.
