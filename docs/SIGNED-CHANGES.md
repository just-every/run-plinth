# Signed change orders

Open an active job’s **Changes** screen to prepare a signed change. The current authenticated builder and one existing assigned client account are the named signers. An agent cannot sign or apply a change.

1. Review the cost code, private estimated cost, client selling price and an existing human-reviewed tax source. The existing allowance workflow can also supply a reviewed priced change; its original allowance, earlier adjustment and incremental amount stay in the document.
2. Review and publish any existing draft schedule tasks first. Choose that priced change, the client and affected booked tasks. Review whole calendar days of impact, including dependent work and the resulting completion date. A conflict, an uncleared prerequisite or a calendar that cannot reconcile those days prevents presentation.
3. Both people open the same immutable document, type their current account name and explicitly consent to sign it electronically. Download the exact document and its attributed signatures. Private cost lines remain in office custody; the client sees the agreed scope, selling price, reviewed tax, allowance and dates.
4. A builder separately reviews and applies the signed terms. This appends a current budget and SOV revision, publishes the exact reviewed dates and prepares a linked billing application and invoice draft. When the job has retainage, explicitly review the new positive line’s work and stored-material rates during application. Existing rates and release milestones remain fixed. For a signed credit with no earned work to bill, choose **Apply credit now and bill later** and record a reason: the signed credit updates the budget, SOV and dates without creating a negative pay app or an invoice. The next earned application includes its full credit and reviewed tax. Original budgets and earlier SOV rows remain fixed. The SOV and invoice sources retain the signed document and both signatures.
5. Review actual earned progress through **Billing** before approving and issuing its application. Invoice issue and an explicitly approved invoice email remain separate actions. Preparing or signing a change never certifies installed work, issues a supplier order, moves money or sends a notice.

Either named person can decline or dispute a presented or signed document before application. Reasons and existing signatures remain in history. A new presentation requires fresh signatures. Changed commercial sources, changed selections, revoked signers and repeated application are refused without partial budget, date or billing writes.

Native archives retain exact documents, private sources, signatures and application identities. Job erasure removes them; a later erasure journal fences restoration of an old archive. Receiving installations establish their own sessions and private settings.

This workflow does not establish delegated signer authority, external accounting completion, processor effects or outbound schedule-change acknowledgments. CLIENT-006, SCH-004, NOTIFY-001, PAY-001 and BILL-006 retain their existing requirements. A mail provider’s acceptance is recorded as acceptance, not proof of recipient delivery.
