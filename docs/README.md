## Who does what

The owner and project managers manage jobs. A client signs in with the email assigned to their job and approves their own variations. The owner manages project-manager access, imports and permanent job erasure. Agent credentials prepare drafts; human sign-in is required for approval and other final actions.

Plinth keeps the accepted quote fixed and retains each approval with its exact terms and the people involved. Purchasing records supplier commitments and deliveries. Progress-claim downloads are working records for your external books; Plinth does not send supplier orders, make payments, calculate tax or prepare statutory payment claims.
