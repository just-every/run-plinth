# Plinth first release

- A builder creates a job, prepares a quote revision and records the client's acceptance; authorised people sign in with ordinary email links.
- A manager schedules trades without double booking and publishes a daily site diary with retrievable photos.
- The job's signed-in client approves an exact variation; a manager issues a supplier PO and records its receipts.
- A manager reviews, approves and exports a reconciled progress claim once, with contract, prior claims, current amount and PO commitments visible. Agents prepare drafts; people approve and their decisions are retained.
- The owner dry-runs, approves, journals and undoes an Accepted jobs CSV import; edited jobs survive undo.
- The owner exports and restores the job archive including photos, or erases a job and its import custody; a later erasure journal prevents restoring an older archive.

Not needed: brand/homepage design, a run site or domain purchase, real provider calls, accounting or payments, statutory payment claims, takeoff/BIM, safety certification, warranty insurance or independent release review.
