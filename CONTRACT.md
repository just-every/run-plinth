# Plinth product contract

Target edition: 17 October 2026. Plinth is the US residential construction system that carries a builder or remodeler from lead and estimate through contract, schedule, selections, subcontractors, field delivery, actual job costing, progress billing and warranty. The destination is the full construction job sold by Buildertrend, Procore, Houzz Pro, JobTread and Ressio, with agents that restore and verify promised dates and margin. New business is USD and US-first; historical AUD records remain labelled and separate.

This contract defines the product being built, not a release claim. The final Status table is implementation truth at the cited commit. The previous unnumbered contract and first-release acceptance promises are retained as NATIVE-001 through NATIVE-014; new clauses extend them. A local test is evidence of its stated flow, not proof of a live provider connection, lossless incumbent migration, physical completion or legal certification. No implementation difficulty narrows the destination.

## Operating rules

Every page, API, file, webhook, automation and export enforces current authority. People approve exact immutable commercial terms; bearer agents prepare drafts and never become decision makers. Source revisions and optimistic versions bind approvals to what was reviewed. Dates and money are explicit; commitments, physical receipt, actual cost, invoice issue and settled payment are separate facts. Corrections add history. Imported records and restored provider work remain inert until an authorized person adopts/reconnects them. External effects retain stable identities and uncertain outcomes remain visible until reconciled. Acceptance scenarios use real application doors, with fakes only at paid/network edges; controlled live-provider and real-export journeys are separately identified when required.

## Published guarantees retained

- **NATIVE-001 — Native sign-in and authority.** People sign in through native email links. Every private page, product handler, photo and import download independently checks current account authority; owner/admin manage jobs and member clients see only jobs matching their email. Revocation and matching-origin checks also apply to direct HTTP calls. **Acceptance evidence:** Invite a manager and two clients through account settings; sign in, read each job and photo through the browser and HTTP, then revoke access. Signed-out, wrong-client and cross-origin requests fail without writes.

- **NATIVE-002 — Quote revisions and recorded acceptance.** A builder prepares numbered quote revisions, records the actual client acceptance method and evidence against the latest revision and activates the job. Accepted scope and price are immutable; later changes need their own decision. This recorded acceptance remains available alongside the target electronic signature journey. **Acceptance evidence:** Create a job through Jobs, prepare two quotes, accept the latest with a reference to the client decision, reload and inspect its fixed lines and acceptance record. Attempt to replace the accepted quote and require refusal.

- **NATIVE-003 — Trade conflict prevention.** Managers book a trade into explicit start/end intervals. Overlapping commitments for the same trade across different jobs are refused, including stale or concurrent bookings, and the conflicting job and interval are shown. **Acceptance evidence:** Book an electrician on one active job, attempt an overlapping booking on another through Trades and the API, and submit concurrent stale mutations. Inspect the conflict and confirm only one compatible committed state persists.

- **NATIVE-004 — Published diaries and original photos.** Managers publish dated site notes and PNG/JPEG photographs; draft entries remain private. Original bounded photo bytes, type, size and checksum are retained and served only to authorized people, with assigned clients limited to published job diaries. **Acceptance evidence:** Prepare a diary/photo as a bearer draft, check that the client cannot retrieve it, publish as a manager and retrieve identical bytes as the client. A different client and unsafe image upload are refused.

- **NATIVE-005 — Client decision on exact variation terms.** Only the assigned signed-in client approves a prepared variation, including a credit. Approval fixes exact wording, amount, preparer, decision maker and time; a credit cannot take the contract below amounts already claimed. Managers and agents cannot stand in for the client. **Acceptance evidence:** Prepare a priced variation through Variations, try manager, bearer and foreign-client approvals, then approve as the assigned client and inspect the immutable snapshot. Retry and attempt a credit below approved claims; neither changes accepted economics.

- **NATIVE-006 — Issued purchase commitments and partial receipts.** A manager issues a prepared supplier PO once, preserving the issuing decision and amount. Append-only receipts consume its outstanding value with a delivery reference and person; over-receipt is refused. Recording the PO does not by itself send or pay the supplier. **Acceptance evidence:** Prepare and issue a PO through Purchasing, receive it in two parts, inspect remaining commitment, and try issuing it twice and exceeding the outstanding value. Read back every receipt and approval.

- **NATIVE-007 — Reconciled cumulative claim export.** Managers review accepted quote, approved variations, prior cumulative claims and supplier commitments/receipts before approving a claim. Approval reserves only its increase, checks current source economics and freezes reconciliation. Export and repeat download return the same JSON/CSV working record without another financial event. **Acceptance evidence:** Prepare a claim through Claims, change a receipt before approval and require a fresh draft. Approve/export two cumulative claims including concurrent export and repeat download; verify prior plus current equals cumulative and no overclaim or duplicate approval occurs.

- **NATIVE-008 — Draft-only bearer authority.** Even a credential minted by the owner identifies an agent with draft preparation authority. Agents cannot create native jobs, accept, approve, issue orders, receive goods, export claims, import or erase; owners can inspect and revoke credentials. **Acceptance evidence:** Mint a credential through People/access API, prepare quote, diary, change and claim drafts, then attempt every protected final action. Revoke it and require further requests to fail.

- **NATIVE-009 — Exact cents and atomic decisions.** Money is checked integer cents, approvals snapshot exact inputs, and optimistic job and authority versions are checked inside a real D1 batch alongside writes. A stale record or revoked actor cannot commit half a decision or overwrite concurrent work. The published ledger uses AUD; US-001 defines the USD destination without relabelling historical money. **Acceptance evidence:** Post fractional/unsafe cent inputs and two writes with the same job version through the job API; require validation or conflict with no partial state. Prepare then change claim sources, and require the approval to be refused.

- **NATIVE-010 — Reviewed accepted-job CSV custody.** An owner previews Accepted jobs CSV, approves its exact fingerprint, retains original bytes and receives an import journal. Import creates inert job/quote drafts and retains source acceptance as unverified evidence. Replays deduplicate; bounded work resumes; undo removes unchanged imports and preserves native edits. **Acceptance evidence:** Use Move in to preview invalid and valid CSV, alter a reviewed file, apply twice, resume a larger import and undo after accepting one job natively. Download originals and compare bytes; edited jobs survive and no imported job activates itself.

- **NATIVE-011 — Private archive round trip.** Owner CLI export and empty-destination restore preserve jobs, approval snapshots, diary photo bytes, source custody, journals and durable account metadata. Sessions, credentials, private settings and local outbox messages are excluded; the receiving installation establishes its own owner and mail settings. **Acceptance evidence:** Run rer export on populated job/import data and rer import into an empty checkout, read back jobs and identical photo bytes and inspect the archive for absent sessions/credentials/settings. Corrupt or nonempty restore is refused.

- **NATIVE-012 — Job erasure and restoration fences.** The owner erases a job, its photo bytes and dependent import custody. Shared import originals containing erased material become unavailable, durable erasure receipts retire old work, and a later erasure journal fences restoration of an older archive. External downloads and already-sent messages remain under their holders' custody. **Acceptance evidence:** Erase through History, then read job, photos, import originals and erasure journal through HTTP. Attempt old import work and rer import of a pre-erasure archive with the later journal; erased records cannot return.

- **NATIVE-013 — Durable human approval history.** Each job retains immutable approval snapshots naming preparer, actual approving person, time and revision. Managers can inspect them in History and archives; client responses remove purchasing details and private claim reconciliation. **Acceptance evidence:** Complete quote, trade, diary, variation, PO and claim decisions, reload History and round-trip an archive. Compare exact snapshots and assigned-client job responses; purchasing records and supplier reconciliation remain private.

- **NATIVE-014 — Separate public run site and installed workspace.** The installation-independent homepage, Run instructions and Markdown guides work without reaching private instance routes. The installed root remains the Lobby, with Plinth identity across setup and workspace and responsive desktop/phone styling. **Acceptance evidence:** Build and serve the static site through HTTP, follow Run and all local guide/assets links, then open the installed Lobby and job workspace at 1440 and 390 pixels with a populated job. Confirm readable controls and separated public/private navigation.

## Target clauses

### US business setup and access

- **US-001 — USD and US construction conventions.** New US companies estimate, contract, cost and bill in USD with checked cents, decimal quantities/units and explicit rounding. US addresses, feet/inches/square feet and metric conversion, US phone numbers, IANA job timezones, working weeks, holidays and daylight saving are supported. Historical AUD records keep their currency and cannot be silently added to USD totals. **Acceptance evidence:** Create a US remodeler and two jobs spanning daylight saving; estimate 12.5 square feet, inspect exact extended cents and local task times, then import an AUD historical job and verify separate currency reporting.

- **CRM-001 — Customers contacts and properties.** Maintain people, households, businesses, multiple owners, billing contacts and sites separately from jobs, with contact history, preferences and external identifiers. One customer can commission several jobs and a job can have multiple authorized client contacts without duplicate addresses. **Acceptance evidence:** Create one household with two decision makers and two sites, start a job at each, update a billing contact and inspect both jobs without losing site history.

- **CRM-002 — Residential lead pipeline and follow-up.** Capture website, referral, Houzz and manual leads, assign an estimator, track qualification, visits, opportunity value, stage and next action, and convert a won proposal into its job without retyping contacts. Each lead keeps its source; Houzz Pro leads arrive through the INT-011 Zapier trigger or the forwarded Houzz lead-notification email parsed into a draft lead, and duplicates of an existing contact are matched for review rather than created twice. Lost leads retain reasons and consent-aware follow-up. **Acceptance evidence:** Submit a website inquiry, deliver a Houzz lead through the INT-011 Zapier connection and the same Houzz lead again by forwarded notification email, and enter a referral naming the referring client. The Houzz lead exists once with source Houzz, the referral credits its client and the lead-source report counts each. Assign a site visit, prepare a proposal and convert the won lead to a job; another lead goes lost and receives no further campaign after opting out.

- **SET-001 — Templates branding and team adoption.** The owner configures business identity, proposal/invoice branding, numbering, approval limits, job templates and reusable checklists and TODO-001 to-do templates (included in job templates or applied alone), plus an optional custom client/sub portal domain (for example portal.builder.com) with verified DNS and automatically issued TLS. Role-specific onboarding brings a crew member, client and subcontractor to their first task without exposing office finance; provider, storage and messaging costs are visible before enabling services. **Acceptance evidence:** Configure a company template, branding and a verified custom portal domain, create a second job from it and invite all three external/field roles. Each reaches the assigned task on a phone through the builder's domain; the owner sees configured service costs and can export configuration.

- **ROLE-001 — Owner administrator and project manager rights.** Owners control installation, billing connectors, access, erasure and restore; delegated administrators manage company records and the custom roles of ROLE-006, while project managers manage only assigned jobs and approvals within owner-set limits. Every internal user holds exactly one preset or custom role plus job assignments, and a role change applies on that user's next request. No project grant confers installation secrets or another project's authority. **Acceptance evidence:** Invite an administrator and project manager, grant one job and a PO limit, then exercise job, PO, connector and erasure routes for each. Over-limit and unassigned actions fail without writes. Reassign the project manager to a custom ROLE-006 role and confirm the next request uses its permissions.

- **ROLE-002 — Estimator and field crew rights.** Estimators work leads, takeoff, bids and proposals but cannot approve client decisions or change paid records. Superintendents and crew see assigned plans, tasks, logs, issues and their time, with hidden client pricing, margins and other employees' pay unless expressly granted. Estimator, superintendent and crew are ROLE-006 presets: their defaults are stated here and an owner can copy and adjust them per feature. **Acceptance evidence:** Sign in as estimator, superintendent and crew on assigned and unassigned jobs. Complete each role's daily work and request private margin, pay and approval routes; unauthorized results remain inaccessible.

- **ROLE-003 — Client and subcontractor portal rights.** Clients see their own contracts, selections, changes, approved schedules, published photos, invoices and payments; subcontractors see only invited scopes, current documents, tasks, their bills and compliance requests. Clients never receive internal markup, vendor bids or internal conversations. Owners adjust the client and subcontractor presets per feature through ROLE-006 (for example hiding a cost-plus budget from clients or letting subs update their task dates), never beyond the job or scope grant. **Acceptance evidence:** Give two clients and two subs separate jobs/scopes, complete a selection and subcontractor upload, then try cross-job identifiers, document URLs and search/export. Each result and action obeys the same grant.

- **ROLE-004 — Accountant reviewer and lender access.** Bookkeepers can code bills, reconcile and administer authorized ledger connections; read-only accountants/lenders inspect only granted financial reports and draw evidence. Approval and payment authority are distinct from data entry, with access revoked immediately. Bookkeeper and reader are ROLE-006 presets an owner can copy and adjust. **Acceptance evidence:** Enter a bill as a bookkeeper, approve as the designated manager and open a lender-only draw packet. Attempts by a reader to alter coding or by an unauthorized clerk to release payment fail.

- **ROLE-005 — Audit search retention and access history.** Every financial mutation, permission change, export, connector send, import and automated action carries actor, source, before/after reference and time in searchable append-only audit history. Owner retention and legal holds cover documents and communications, with explained erasure exceptions and secret-free export. **Acceptance evidence:** Change a role, recode a bill, issue an invoice, send a connector request and search/export their events. Place a job on hold, request erasure and see a reasoned hold outcome; remove the hold and verify only retained audit metadata remains.

- **ROLE-006 — Custom roles and per-feature permissions.** Owners and administrators create a role by copying any preset (administrator, project manager, estimator, superintendent, crew, bookkeeper, reader, client or subcontractor), renaming it and switching view, create, edit, delete and approve separately for each feature area: leads, estimates and proposals, budgets and cost-to-complete, bills, purchase and work orders, change orders, selections, schedule, daily logs, time, documents and RFIs, invoices and payments, reports and company settings. Financial visibility (cost, margin, markup and pay rates) is its own switch, approve permissions carry USD limits, and job assignment narrows any role. Every page, API, export, search, report, notification and agent answer resolves the same permission. Editing a role or moving a user to another role takes effect on the next request, is audited under ROLE-005 and never changes past approvals. An editor cannot grant a permission they do not hold, and the owner role cannot be deleted or edited away. **Acceptance evidence:** Copy the superintendent preset to "Lead Carpenter – no financials" with budget and cost view off, and the project manager preset to "Office Manager" with PO approve on up to $5,000. Assign two existing users. As each user, exercise budget, PO, bill, schedule and daily-log routes, CSV/PDF exports, global search, notifications and the forecast report: hidden budgets and margins are absent everywhere, a $4,000 PO approves, and a $6,000 PO is refused without writes. Turn budget view on, verify the next request shows it, and inspect the audit entries. An administrator attempting to grant a permission they lack is refused.

### Estimating bids and contract formation

- **EST-001 — Cost codes quantities and estimate revisions.** Estimate labor, material, equipment, subcontract and other costs by cost code, phase, quantity and unit, with separate cost and selling price, alternates, inclusions/exclusions and version comparison. Decimal quantities and conversions are exact; revision changes identify affected scope and totals. **Acceptance evidence:** Build a multi-trade estimate with fractional measurements, alternate finishes and cost/sell rates, revise quantities and compare versions. Export cost-code totals and independently reconcile rounded line costs and sell prices.

- **EST-002 — Reusable catalogs assemblies and templates.** Maintain dated vendor rates, cost catalogs, phase groups and assemblies combining labor/material productivity and waste. Copy a house/remodel template into a job, change dimensions and review rate updates without rewriting approved estimates. **Acceptance evidence:** Create a wall assembly with waste and labor production, price it on two jobs, change a supplier rate and inspect the impact preview. Only approved draft updates take effect; the sold estimate stays unchanged.

- **EST-003 — Markups margin discounts and sales tax.** Calculate line/group/project overhead, markup versus gross-margin pricing, discounts and taxable basis in a visible order with exact rounding. US jurisdictional sales/use tax and exemptions use approved rates or an authorized Stripe Tax/Avalara connection; tax snapshots stay with proposals, bills and invoices. **Acceptance evidence:** Price the same estimate using markup and target margin, apply a discount and a reviewed taxable/exempt split, and compare calculations. A rate change requires a new preview and never mutates signed totals; unavailable provider calculation blocks claims of verified tax.

- **EST-004 — Drawing-based digital quantity takeoff.** Upload calibrated PDF plans, measure lengths/areas/counts, combine sheets and deduct openings. Measurements retain sheet revision, scale, annotations, waste and links to estimate assemblies; a revised drawing flags measurements for human review rather than silently repricing sold work. **Acceptance evidence:** Calibrate a plan, measure walls/floor/openings and populate estimate lines. Replace its sheet revision and inspect changed measures and priced differences before accepting a revision.

- **EST-005 — Supplier product capture and specification boards.** Capture products from supplier URLs or authorized feeds with SKU, source link, dimensions, image, quoted price, lead time and capture date. Room/finish boards compare alternatives and feed selection/estimate drafts; stale prices and discontinued products are visible before ordering. **Acceptance evidence:** Capture a faucet and cabinet finish, arrange a client room board, change the supplier price and select an alternative. Verify refreshed values require review and the final selection retains its source evidence.

- **EST-006 — Branded electronic proposals and contracts.** Send branded fixed-price, cost-plus and time/material proposals with options, payment milestones, exclusions, attachments and expiry. Authorized client signers receive exact immutable documents, consent and signature evidence, can decline or request revision, and download a complete signed copy. **Acceptance evidence:** Send a proposal to two authorized signers, revise one unsent option, sign the current version through the client portal and download the signed artifact. Expired links and superseded versions cannot execute.

- **EST-007 — Signed estimate becomes budget and billing plan.** Accepting the exact proposal creates a frozen original cost-code budget, contract revenue, allowances, commitments plan, schedule template and billing/SOV baseline. Accepted changes update current budget/revenue separately, preserving original versus revised comparisons without duplicate jobs or charges. **Acceptance evidence:** Sign an estimate, open the resulting budget/schedule/SOV, approve a later change and inspect original versus revised amounts and next invoice. Replay acceptance and prove one job and one baseline.

- **EST-008 — Room design and client visualization handoff.** Clients and builders organize floor plans, room measurements, mood boards, finish images and 3D walkthrough/render links alongside selections. Import/export common PDF, JPEG/PNG and GLB/OBJ/IFC artifacts through authorized design tools, keeping design revisions and chosen products linked to priced construction scope. **Acceptance evidence:** Bring a room board exported as PDF and JPEG images plus its 3D model as GLB and OBJ files into a job, confirm each file opens with its revision recorded, annotate the selected finish, open a linked walkthrough on a phone and compare its approved costed selection with the construction plan. A later design revision does not replace the signed specification.

- **BID-001 — Bid requests and subcontractor responses.** Invite qualified subs to priced or unpriced bid packages with scoped cost codes, versioned plans, due dates and addenda. Subs acknowledge revisions, ask questions and submit itemized prices, exclusions and attachments privately through their portal; owners track decline, receipt and lateness. **Acceptance evidence:** Invite three subs, issue a plan addendum, receive two priced responses and one decline. Each sub sees only its scope/response and the estimator sees who acknowledged the revision.

- **BID-002 — Comparable scope and award decisions.** Level bids side by side by cost code, quantity, allowance, tax, exclusions, alternates and duration; flag missing scope and normalize comparison without altering the original bid. A human award retains the comparison and creates the approved commitment and budget adjustment once. **Acceptance evidence:** Compare bids with different exclusions and units, add a reviewed normalization and award one. Inspect original bids, decision evidence, resulting commitment and budget delta; another award requires explicit replacement/cancellation.

- **BID-003 — Bid follow-up and procurement lead times.** Bid deadlines and material lead times connect to required-on-site dates and schedule tasks. Approved reminders chase unacknowledged packages and missing prices, stop on response/decline and escalate unresolved gaps to the estimator before a start date is promised. **Acceptance evidence:** Set a long-lead window package and bid deadline, advance time and receive one sub response. Inspect deduplicated reminders and the remaining escalation; the answered sub receives no further chase.

### Scheduling and field delivery

- **SCH-001 — Gantt tasks dependencies and milestones.** Plan jobs in Gantt, calendar and list views with owners, durations, milestones, predecessor relationships, lag, working calendars and critical path. Move a predecessor and preview dependent shifts, conflicts and client completion impact before publishing. Cycles are refused. **Acceptance evidence:** Create demolition-to-inspection-to-drywall dependencies, add a permit milestone, delay inspection and compare all views. Publish the reviewed dates and reject a dependency cycle.

- **SCH-002 — Schedule baselines templates and variance.** Reuse a schedule template on sale, record a human-approved baseline and compare actual starts/finishes and forecasts with that baseline. Approved changes create named revisions; history still shows original promised dates and why they moved. **Acceptance evidence:** Apply a remodel template when a job sells, baseline it, record delayed framing and publish a revised finish. Compare baseline/current/actual and open the authorizing decision.

- **SCH-003 — Cross-job capacity and trade confirmations.** See trade crews, equipment and availability across jobs with conflict checks, leave and travel buffers. Invitations request explicit subcontractor confirmation or alternative dates; an unconfirmed booking stays visibly at risk rather than assuming attendance. **Acceptance evidence:** Schedule a crew on two sites with travel and absence constraints, request confirmation and receive an alternative. Confirm only a feasible slot and inspect other-job conflict visibility.

- **SCH-004 — Published schedule changes and acknowledgements.** Publishing a schedule revision tells affected trades, crew and clients what changed and requires acknowledgement when their work moved, delivered through each recipient's NOTIFY-001 channels with the moved-task notice required for assigned trades. Delivery, acknowledgement and unresolved exceptions are distinct; escalation follows the owner's rule until a replacement date is confirmed. **Acceptance evidence:** Move an approved task, publish once, deliver to two trades (one by app push, one by SMS) and a client by email, acknowledge one and fail one delivery. Inspect the revision diff, remaining escalation and no notices to unaffected recipients.

- **SCH-005 — Mobile daily work and look-ahead.** A field worker opens today's assigned work and a rolling look-ahead with site address, contacts, current drawings, safety/inspection prerequisites, materials due and their own open and overdue TODO-001 checklist items. Updates to task status and installed quantities retain actual time and evidence for schedule/cost forecasting. **Acceptance evidence:** At 390 pixels open a superintendent's daily plan, find the current sheet, complete an assigned to-do checklist item, record progress and inspect tomorrow's dependencies and updated office forecast. Unassigned job data stays hidden.

- **SCH-006 — Inspection permit and weather constraints.** Track jurisdiction, permit documents, inspection requests/results and weather/site constraints as dependencies with accountable people. Failed inspection or unsafe-weather hold blocks affected tasks until a human records clearance; agents cannot certify compliance or physical completion. **Acceptance evidence:** Require an inspection before drywall, record failure and a rain hold, attempt to publish dependent work, then attach passed inspection evidence and reschedule. The history retains every hold and release.

- **DOC-001 — Versioned plans specifications and distribution.** Store plan sets, specifications and addenda with sheet/revision numbers, issue dates, supersession and distribution acknowledgements. Field users land on the latest approved revision while older marked-up versions remain available as history; pinned links identify exact sheets. **Acceptance evidence:** Upload two plan sets sharing sheet numbers, approve the second and notify its recipients. Open a pinned old-sheet link and the current drawing on a phone; each is clearly labelled and annotations retain their source revision.

- **DOC-002 — Drawing recognition hyperlinks and comparison.** Extract sheet names/numbers, propose cross-sheet/detail hyperlinks and show revision overlays/change areas. A reviewer corrects uncertain recognition before publication; extracted text and annotations are searchable and never overwrite source drawing bytes. **Acceptance evidence:** Upload a scanned multi-sheet PDF with a misread sheet number, review/correct recognition, follow a detail link and compare a revised sheet. Download originals and verify unchanged bytes and revision-specific markup.

- **DOC-003 — RFI questions responses and resolution.** Field staff and subs raise RFIs with sheet/cost-code/task references, assignee, due date and attachments. Authorized design/owner responses are revisioned, distributed and acknowledged; a response with cost/date impact prepares a change and does not itself authorize work. **Acceptance evidence:** Raise a clash RFI from a sheet, route it to the designer, answer with a revised detail and flag a cost impact. Confirm affected trades see the response while the extra work waits for the client change decision.

- **DOC-004 — Submittals approvals and procurement release.** Manage submittal registers, required dates, vendor documents, reviewers and dispositions including revise/resubmit. Review cycles retain exact versions and annotations; approval releases only its matching product/commitment and flags deviations from specified scope. **Acceptance evidence:** Submit a window package, reject it with comments, resubmit and approve. Inspect both cycles, notify the supplier and confirm only the approved version can satisfy the procurement gate.

- **DOC-005 — Meetings correspondence and decision search.** Record meeting agendas/minutes, attendees, decisions and actions with owners/due dates. Job email, comments and attached files are searchable by text, tag, sender, sheet and cost code under the same access rules; resolved answers link to the work they changed. **Acceptance evidence:** Record a site meeting, assign an action and capture a related inbound email. Search its decision from the job, complete the action and verify a client search cannot reveal private sub bid correspondence.

- **DOC-006 — Model coordination and design-system connection.** Link authorized Autodesk Docs/BIM Collaborate model versions, IFC coordination views and issues to jobs, RFIs, sheets and cost codes. Coordinate design/cost consequences across projects and export issue/evidence references to specialist systems; large model processing runs in bounded background work. **Acceptance evidence:** Connect a permitted Autodesk project, open a versioned model issue on site, raise an RFI and compare cost impact across two affected jobs. Revoking Autodesk access stops refresh and never exposes another project.

- **DOC-007 — Job document folders and selective sharing.** Each job has Documents, Photos and Videos areas with nested folders for miscellaneous job files such as signed contracts, permits, insurance certificates, warranties, manuals and correspondence, beside the structured plans of DOC-001. Folders and files are private to internal users by default and are shared with the job's client, with all of its subs or with chosen sub companies one folder or file at a time under ROLE-003/ROLE-006 grants; sharing is visible on the folder and revocable. Uploads keep original bytes, uploader, time and versions, can be searched by name and tag, and can be attached to any job record or to CARE-001 handover. Company document folders hold templates and standard files outside any job. **Acceptance evidence:** On a job create Contracts, Permits and Warranties folders, upload a signed contract PDF, a permit and a warranty PDF, share Permits with the plumbing sub and Warranties with the client. The sub sees only Permits and the client only Warranties, a second job's sub sees neither, and nobody external sees Contracts. Upload a new contract version, unshare Permits and confirm the sub loses access on the next request. Download each file and verify unchanged bytes, then add the warranty PDF to the handover package.

- **FIELD-001 — Daily logs photos comments and production.** Crew record daily weather, headcounts, work performed, quantities, delays, equipment, deliveries and annotated photos/video, with comments and private/client publication controls. Managers review corrections as additional history and link field progress to tasks and cost codes. Weather is pre-filled for the site and date from a named source (the US National Weather Service API by default) and labelled with it; a crew correction keeps both values. **Acceptance evidence:** From a phone enter a daily log with quantities and photos, add a sub comment, publish a client-safe version and correct a quantity and the pre-filled weather. Inspect retained history and schedule/cost inputs in the office.

- **FIELD-002 — Field time crew approval and cost allocation.** Employees clock in/out or enter time by job, cost code and activity with breaks, corrections and supervisor approval. Crew timesheets, overtime classification and burden rates allocate approved labor cost; location capture is consented and visible, never a claim that GPS proves work. Optional per-site geofence reminders prompt a consenting worker to clock in or out on arrival and departure; a reminder never records time by itself. **Acceptance evidence:** Enter a crew day split across two jobs and codes with a break and correction, approve it, inspect exact labor/burden costs and payroll export. Another worker cannot inspect pay rates or approve their own time. Enable a site geofence for a consenting worker, arrive and leave: reminders appear and no time is recorded until the worker acts, and a non-consenting worker has no location captured.

- **FIELD-003 — Offline mobile capture and conflict resolution.** iOS and Android apps serve field, client and subcontractor logins under their ROLE-003/ROLE-006 grants, receive NOTIFY-002 push, and retain granted current plans, tasks, TODO-001 to-dos and contacts offline and queue logs, photos, time, to-do checklist completions and punch updates with visible sync state. Reconnection deduplicates uploads, identifies stale revisions/conflicts and enforces revocation before server writes; disconnected approval cannot authorize changed terms. **Acceptance evidence:** Cache an assigned job on each phone platform, disconnect, log work/time/photos, update the plan in the office and reconnect. Verify each item once, resolve the stale-plan conflict and refuse queued writes from a revoked user. Sign in to the same app as a client and as a sub and complete one portal action each.

- **FIELD-004 — Punch lists issues and configurable forms.** Create annotated punch/quality/safety observations and reusable field forms with assignee, severity, due date and location/sheet references. Subs submit repair evidence, managers inspect/reject/accept, and clients see only released closeout items. Captured checklists support qualified review rather than issuing safety certification. **Acceptance evidence:** Walk a job on a phone, create an issue from a photo, assign a trade, reject its first repair and accept the second with evidence. Search/export open items and confirm form answers and both attempts remain.

- **FIELD-005 — Deliveries inventory and equipment at the job.** Track material quantities ordered, received, damaged, returned and installed by location/cost code, with delivery tickets and equipment allocation. Partial value receipts remain available, while quantity discrepancies generate a supplier claim and forecast impact rather than silently changing the order. **Acceptance evidence:** Receive half a material order with damage, transfer good stock to another site and record installation. Reconcile quantity/value balances and equipment dates with the PO and cost forecast.

- **TODO-001 — Job to-dos with assignable checklists.** Internal users create to-dos on a job or at company level with no job, each with title, notes, due date and time, priority, tags, attachments and a checklist whose items are each assigned to an internal user, a subcontractor contact or a client contact under their ROLE-003/ROLE-006 grants and job assignment. A to-do links to a schedule task, daily log, RFI, selection or change order and appears on that record. To-dos are created ad hoc, from SET-001 to-do templates or by a job template (with due dates relative to the job or linked task). People comment, complete single items or the whole to-do, and reopen; each completion and reopening records who and when.
