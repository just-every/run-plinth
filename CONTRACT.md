# Plinth contract

The first release is defined by ACCEPTANCE.md. Every product HTTP handler independently checks current native account authority. Owner/admin manage jobs; members are clients limited to their matching email; bearer credentials can prepare drafts only. Immutable approval snapshots bind decisions to exact terms. Amounts are integer AUD cents and claim approvals reserve cumulative positions atomically. D1 migrations preserve the job ledger, photos, import custody and erasure history. Provider effects and statutory claims are outside this release.
