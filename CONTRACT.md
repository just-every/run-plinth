# Plinth product contract

Target edition: 17 October 2026. Plinth is the US residential construction system that carries a builder or remodeler from lead and estimate through contract, schedule, selections, subcontractors, field delivery, actual job costing, progress billing and warranty. The destination is the full construction job sold by Buildertrend, Procore, Houzz Pro, JobTread and Ressio, with agents that restore and verify promised dates and margin. New business is USD and US-first; historical AUD records remain labeled and separate.

This contract defines the product being built, not a release claim. The final Status table is implementation truth at the cited commit. The previous unnumbered contract and first-release acceptance promises are retained as NATIVE-001 through NATIVE-014; new clauses extend them. A local test is evidence of its stated flow, not proof of a live provider connection, lossless incumbent migration, physical completion or legal certification. No implementation difficulty narrows the destination.

## Operating rules

Every page, API, file, webhook, automation and export enforces current authority. People approve exact immutable commercial terms; bearer agents prepare drafts and never become decision makers. Source revisions and optimistic versions bind approvals to what was reviewed. Dates and money are explicit; commitments, physical receipt, actual cost, invoice issue and settled payment are separate facts. Corrections add history. Imported records and restored provider work remain inert until an authorized person adopts/reconnects them. External effects retain stable identities and uncertain outcomes remain visible until reconciled. Acceptance scenarios use real application doors, with fakes only at paid/network edges; controlled live-provider and real-export journeys are separately identified when required.

## Published guarantees retained

- **NATIVE-001 — Native sign-in and authority.** People sign in through native email links. Every private page, product handler, photo and import download independently checks current account authority; owner/admin manage jobs and member clients see only jobs matching their original client email or an explicitly authorized CRM-001 contact. Revocation and matching-origin checks also apply to direct HTTP calls. **Acceptance evidence:** Invite a manager and two clients through account settings; sign in, read each job and photo through the browser and HTTP, then revoke access. Signed-out, wrong-client and cross-origin requests fail without writes.

- **NATIVE-002 — Quote revisions and recorded acceptance.** A builder prepares numbered quote revisions, records the actual client acceptance method and evidence against the latest revision and activates the job. Accepted scope and price are immutable; later changes need their own decision. This recorded acceptance remains available alongside the target electronic signature journey. **Acceptance evidence:** Create a job through Jobs, prepare two quotes, accept the latest with a reference to the client decision, reload and inspect its fixed lines and acceptance record. Attempt to replace the accepted quote and require refusal.

- **NATIVE-003 — Trade conflict prevention.** Managers book a trade into explicit start/end intervals. Overlapping commitments for the same trade across different jobs are refused, including stale or concurrent bookings, and the conflicting job and interval are shown. **Acceptance evidence:** Book an electrician on one active job, attempt an overlapping booking on another through Trades and the API, and submit concurrent stale mutations. Inspect the conflict and confirm only one compatible committed state persists.

- **NATIVE-004 — Published diaries and original photos.** Managers publish dated site notes and PNG/JPEG photographs; draft entries remain private. Original bounded photo bytes, type, size and checksum are retained and served only to authorized people, with assigned clients limited to published job diaries. **Acceptance evidence:** Prepare a diary/photo as a bearer draft, check that the client cannot retrieve it, publish as a manager and retrieve identical bytes as the client. A different client and unsafe image upload are refused.

- **NATIVE-005 — Client decision on exact variation terms.** Only an assigned signed-in client (the original email or an authorized CRM-001 contact) approves a prepared variation, including a credit. Approval fixes exact wording, amount, preparer, decision maker and time; a credit cannot take the contract below amounts already claimed. Managers and agents cannot stand in for the client. **Acceptance evidence:** Prepare a priced variation through Variations, try manager, bearer and foreign-client approvals, then approve as the assigned client and inspect the immutable snapshot. Retry and attempt a credit below approved claims; neither changes accepted economics.

- **NATIVE-006 — Issued purchase commitments and partial receipts.** A manager issues a prepared supplier PO once, preserving the issuing decision and amount. Append-only receipts consume its outstanding value with a delivery reference and person; over-receipt is refused. Recording the PO does not by itself send or pay the supplier. **Acceptance evidence:** Prepare and issue a PO through Purchasing, receive it in two parts, inspect remaining commitment, and try issuing it twice and exceeding the outstanding value. Read back every receipt and approval.

- **NATIVE-007 — Reconciled cumulative claim export.** Managers review accepted quote, approved variations, prior cumulative claims and supplier commitments/receipts before approving a claim. Approval reserves only its increase, checks current source economics and freezes reconciliation. Export and repeat download return the same JSON/CSV working record without another financial event. **Acceptance evidence:** Prepare a claim through Claims, change a receipt before approval and require a fresh draft. Approve/export two cumulative claims including concurrent export and repeat download; verify prior plus current equals cumulative and no overclaim or duplicate approval occurs.

- **NATIVE-008 — Draft-only bearer authority.** Even a credential minted by the owner identifies an agent with draft preparation authority. Agents cannot create native jobs, accept, approve, issue orders, receive goods, export claims, import or erase; owners can inspect and revoke credentials. **Acceptance evidence:** Mint a credential through People/access API, prepare quote, diary, change and claim drafts, then attempt every protected final action. Revoke it and require further requests to fail.

- **NATIVE-009 — Exact cents and atomic decisions.** Money is checked integer cents, approvals snapshot exact inputs, and optimistic job and authority versions are checked inside a real D1 batch alongside writes. A stale record or revoked actor cannot commit half a decision or overwrite concurrent work. The published ledger uses USD; US-001 defines the remaining US conventions and preservation of historical currency. **Acceptance evidence:** Post fractional/unsafe cent inputs and two writes with the same job version through the job API; require validation or conflict with no partial state. Prepare then change claim sources, and require the approval to be refused.

- **NATIVE-010 — Reviewed accepted-job CSV custody.** An owner previews Accepted jobs CSV, approves its exact fingerprint, retains original bytes and receives an import journal. Import creates inert job/quote drafts and retains source acceptance as unverified evidence. Replays deduplicate; bounded work resumes; undo removes unchanged imports and preserves native edits. **Acceptance evidence:** Use Move in to preview invalid and valid CSV, alter a reviewed file, apply twice, resume a larger import and undo after accepting one job natively. Download originals and compare bytes; edited jobs survive and no imported job activates itself.

- **NATIVE-011 — Private archive round trip.** Owner CLI export and empty-destination restore preserve jobs, approval snapshots, diary photo bytes, source custody, journals and durable account metadata. Sessions, credentials, private settings and local outbox messages are excluded; the receiving installation establishes its own owner and mail settings. **Acceptance evidence:** Run rer export on populated job/import data and rer import into an empty checkout, read back jobs and identical photo bytes and inspect the archive for absent sessions/credentials/settings. Corrupt or nonempty restore is refused.

- **NATIVE-012 — Job erasure and restoration fences.** The owner erases a job, its photo bytes and dependent import custody. Shared import originals containing erased material become unavailable, durable erasure receipts retire old work, and a later erasure journal fences restoration of an older archive. External downloads and already-sent messages remain under their holders' custody. **Acceptance evidence:** Erase through History, then read job, photos, import originals and erasure journal through HTTP. Attempt old import work and rer import of a pre-erasure archive with the later journal; erased records cannot return.

- **NATIVE-013 — Durable human approval history.** Each job retains immutable approval snapshots naming preparer, actual approving person, time and revision. Managers can inspect them in History and archives; client responses remove purchasing details and private claim reconciliation. **Acceptance evidence:** Complete quote, trade, diary, variation, PO and claim decisions, reload History and round-trip an archive. Compare exact snapshots and assigned-client job responses; purchasing records and supplier reconciliation remain private.

- **NATIVE-014 — Separate public run site and installed workspace.** The installation-independent homepage, Run instructions and Markdown guides work without reaching private instance routes. The installed root remains the Lobby, with Plinth identity across setup and workspace and responsive desktop/phone styling. **Acceptance evidence:** Build and serve the static site through HTTP, follow Run and all local guide/assets links, then open the installed Lobby and job workspace at 1440 and 390 pixels with a populated job. Confirm readable controls and separated public/private navigation.

## Target clauses

### US business setup and access

- **US-001 — USD and US construction conventions.** New US companies estimate, contract, cost and bill in USD with checked cents, decimal quantities/units and explicit rounding. US addresses, feet/inches/square feet and metric conversion, US phone numbers, IANA job timezones, working weeks, holidays and daylight saving are supported. Historical AUD records keep their currency and cannot be silently added to USD totals. Exact semantics: the owner or an administrator records company setup (agents read it; clients cannot) with currency fixed to USD, a default IANA time zone, a US postal address (USPS state, DC or territory code; 5-digit or ZIP+4) and a North American Numbering Plan phone stored in E.164. Each job keeps the IANA zone it was created in (given, else the company default, else UTC). Working weekdays, hours and holidays are local wall-clock values on the job's local dates, so a daylight-saving change moves UTC instants and never local hours; a local time skipped by a spring-forward gap resolves to the later wall time (02:30 becomes 03:30) and a repeated fall-back time to its first occurrence. Schedule screens show and accept times in the job zone with the instant's abbreviation. Quantities are exact decimal strings; in, ft, yd, mm, cm, m and sq in, sq ft, sq yd, sq m convert automatically by exact definition (1 in = 0.0254 m) with the factor recorded on the line, other unit pairs need an explicit exact factor, and each line multiplies every factor before rounding half-up once to the cent. Job site and property addresses are kept as written; a contact phone that is a NANP number also carries its E.164 form and any other number stays as written. Historical AUD jobs arrive only through the accepted-jobs CSV `currency` column, stay AUD in every view and export, are refused USD catalog rates and tax, and are reported in their own currency total beside USD, never added to it. Each later clause that adds a money record or report (COST-001–COST-007, BILL-001–BILL-006, PAY-001–PAY-004, RPT-001–RPT-004) or a dated door (SCH-003–SCH-005, FIELD-001–FIELD-005, NOTIFY-001, INT-004, INT-005) inherits these rules before it ships. **Acceptance evidence:** Create a US remodeler and two jobs spanning daylight saving; estimate 12.5 square feet, inspect exact extended cents and local task times, then import an AUD historical job and verify separate currency reporting.

- **CRM-001 — Customers contacts and properties.** Maintain people, households, businesses, multiple owners, billing contacts and sites separately from jobs, with contact history, preferences and external identifiers. One customer can commission several jobs and a job can have multiple authorized client contacts without duplicate addresses. Owners/admins maintain this directory and explicitly link a job to a customer, property and authorized contact IDs; clients and agents cannot manage the directory or grants. Every authorized contact receives the same existing client-safe job, photo, decision and calendar access, checked against current contact email and job grants on each request; the original client email stays authorized. Grant additions/removals record the acting person and time. A job copies its site address when linked to a property and retains that snapshot when the property or billing contact changes. Contact history retains prior edits and dated, attributed conversation notes; preferences and external references stay on their reusable records. CLIENT-006 lets an assigned client choose an existing eligible contact for one revision without granting customer-directory or job access. **Acceptance evidence:** Create one household with two decision makers and two sites, start a job at each, update a billing contact and inspect both jobs without losing site history.

- **CRM-002 — Residential lead pipeline and follow-up.** Capture website, referral, Houzz and manual leads, assign an estimator, track qualification, visits, opportunity value, stage and next action, and convert a won proposal into its job without retyping contacts. Each lead keeps its source; Houzz Pro leads arrive through the INT-011 Zapier trigger or the forwarded Houzz lead-notification email parsed into a draft lead, and duplicates of an existing contact are matched for review rather than created twice. Lost leads retain reasons and consent-aware follow-up. **Acceptance evidence:** Submit a website inquiry, deliver a Houzz lead through the INT-011 Zapier connection and the same Houzz lead again by forwarded notification email, and enter a referral naming the referring client. The Houzz lead exists once with source Houzz, the referral credits its client and the lead-source report counts each. Assign a site visit, prepare a proposal and convert the won lead to a job; another lead goes lost and receives no further campaign after opting out.

- **SET-001 — Templates branding and team adoption.** The owner configures business identity, proposal/invoice branding, numbering, approval limits, job templates and reusable checklists and TODO-001 to-do templates (included in job templates or applied alone), plus an optional custom client/sub portal domain (for example portal.builder.com) with verified DNS and automatically issued TLS. Role-specific onboarding brings a crew member, client and subcontractor to their first task without exposing office finance; provider, storage and messaging costs are visible before enabling services. **Acceptance evidence:** Configure a company template, branding and a verified custom portal domain, create a second job from it and invite all three external/field roles. Each reaches the assigned task on a phone through the builder's domain; the owner sees configured service costs and can export configuration.

- **ROLE-001 — Owner administrator and project manager rights.** Owners control installation, billing connectors, access, erasure and restore; delegated administrators manage company records and the custom roles of ROLE-006, while project managers manage only assigned jobs and approvals within owner-set limits. Every internal user holds exactly one preset or custom role plus job assignments, and a role change applies on that user's next request. No project grant confers installation secrets or another project's authority. Exact rules: the presets today are owner, administrator and project manager (clients are ROLE-003's). A project manager is an operator-auth administrator whom the owner narrows in Plinth's own role table to chosen jobs and a PO approval limit in USD cents; the table is read on every request and every write commits only while the role and assignments it was authorized with still hold, so a change applies on the next request. Only the owner sets a role, assignments or a limit (each change is audited), invites administrators, mints or revokes agent credentials, erases a job and restores an archive; billing connectors do not exist yet and INT-001, INT-002 and INT-003 inherit owner-only control. Administrators manage company records (company setup, vendors, compliance rules, waiver wording, catalogs, pricing, customers, templates) and every job, and may invite clients as NATIVE-001 allows. A project manager sees and works only assigned jobs (others are absent from lists and answer 404, including calendar feeds, which are rechecked on every fetch), prepares and approves job work there, and issues a PO only when its amount is at or below the limit (403 with no write otherwise); they are refused company records, subcontract awards and lien waivers, job creation, erasure, agent credentials and People & access (403). ROLE-006 inherits the custom-role reassignment acceptance point: moving a user to a custom role uses its permissions on the next request. **Acceptance evidence:** Invite an administrator and project manager, grant one job and a PO limit, then exercise job, PO, connector and erasure routes for each. Over-limit and unassigned actions fail without writes. Reassign the project manager to another preset role and back, confirming each next request uses its permissions (the custom ROLE-006 reassignment is proved under ROLE-006).

- **ROLE-002 — Estimator and field crew rights.** Estimators work leads, takeoff, bids and proposals but cannot approve client decisions or change paid records. Superintendents and crew see assigned plans, tasks, logs, issues and their time, with hidden client pricing, margins and other employees' pay unless expressly granted. Estimator, superintendent and crew are ROLE-006 presets: their defaults are stated here and an owner can copy and adjust them per feature. Exact rules: each preset is an operator-auth administrator whom only the owner narrows, in ROLE-001's role table, to chosen jobs (the PO limit is always zero); the table is read on every request and unassigned jobs answer 404 without a write. Before any door runs, a request outside the preset's own list answers 403, so time, pay, billing and report doors that later clauses add stay closed to these presets until the clause registers them: FIELD-002, COST-003 and INT-010 inherit own-time-only for crew and no one else's pay, and ROLE-006's "expressly granted" is the per-feature switch. A lead is a job still in quoting until CRM-002 adds its pipeline, whose doors inherit this estimator scope. The estimator sees assigned jobs' quote revisions with cost and margin, drawings and takeoff, bids and the company rates and catalogs (read only); prepares quote revisions, takeoff, pricing and bid-package drafts; and is refused client acceptance, change orders, selections, purchase orders, invoices, receipts, claims, subcontract awards, bid publication and invitations, rate changes, company records and People & access. A superintendent and crew see the assigned job's booked schedule, drawings, published logs, punch observations and their own checklist items, and never the client's contact, quotes, budgets, invoices, margins or anyone's pay; a superintendent also saves daily-log drafts and captures punch observations, which an owner, administrator or project manager publishes or assigns; crew complete only their own checklist items. **Acceptance evidence:** Sign in as estimator, superintendent and crew on assigned and unassigned jobs. Complete each role's daily work and request private margin, pay and approval routes; unauthorized results remain inaccessible.

- **ROLE-003 — Client and subcontractor portal rights.** Clients see their own contracts, selections, changes, approved schedules, published photos, invoices and payments; subcontractors see only invited scopes, current documents, tasks, their bills and compliance requests. Clients never receive internal markup, vendor bids or internal conversations. Owners adjust the client and subcontractor presets per feature through ROLE-006 (for example hiding a cost-plus budget from clients or letting subs update their task dates), never beyond the job or scope grant. Subcontractor logins inherit SUB-001's directory rule: a sub contact sees only its own company's awarded scopes and never another company's W-9, rates or contacts. Current implementation default (pending ct confirmation of the pre-award exception): BID-001 may separately invite one active, currently qualified company contact to selected bid cost codes before award; that invitation grants only its private bid package, revisions and responses, never an awarded scope, work release or general job access. A current job client is never also treated as a subcontractor on that same job, even if a directory contact overlaps; the client cannot use that overlap to retrieve vendor bids or private company originals. An awarded company's current contacts use the scoped records screen and its JSON doors for their own bills and compliance submissions, search, exports and originals; bills are received, not approved or paid, and compliance uploads enter the existing designated-reviewer queue unverified. Compliance evidence may be supplied for an awarded scope still on hold; billing requires release. The owner can disable existing external features per job through the audited portal-policy door; a feature only subtracts from current client/company/record grants and every next request, file, export, feed and current to-do or invoice send rechecks it. ROLE-006 inherits installation-wide and custom-role permission controls; SUB-002, DOC-001, DOC-007, COST-002, PAY-003, FIELD-003 and NOTIFY-001 inherit these current grant checks before adding records, interfaces or delivery channels. **Acceptance evidence:** Give two clients and two subs separate jobs/scopes, complete a selection and subcontractor upload, then try cross-job identifiers, document URLs and search/export. Each result and action obeys the same grant.

- **ROLE-004 — Accountant reviewer and lender access.** Bookkeepers can code bills, reconcile and administer authorized ledger connections; read-only accountants and lenders inspect only granted financial reports and draw evidence. Approval and payment authority are distinct from data entry, with access revoked immediately. Bookkeeper and reader are ROLE-006 presets an owner can copy and adjust. Exact rules: only the owner assigns job-scoped bookkeeper and reader presets or their custom copies. A bookkeeper enters, recodes and reviews bill discrepancies and records a ledger reconciliation; the designated current manager posts the bill under its separate approval policy. Neither data entry nor reader access grants payment release or period close. A reader sees only an assigned job identity and its allow-listed customer draw packet, with approved application amounts and evidence and issued invoice totals; supplier originals, internal costs, private contacts and operational records are absent. Current grants are checked on every request and fenced at writes. The owner separately designates an active assigned bookkeeper account as accountant reviewer for a job. That account confirms an exact current period snapshot under its own session; the owner alone records the subsequent close. A changed snapshot, revoked designation or removed role grant prevents closure. No professional certification is claimed. INT-001, INT-002, INT-003 and INT-013 inherit this separate explicit authorization before supplying ledger connections; PAY-003 inherits separate payment authority. **Acceptance evidence:** Enter a bill as a bookkeeper, approve as the designated manager and open a lender-only draw packet. Attempts by a reader to alter coding or by an unauthorized clerk to release payment fail.

- **ROLE-005 — Audit search retention and access history.** Every financial mutation, permission change, export, connector send, import and automated action carries actor, source, before and after reference and time in searchable append-only audit history. Owner retention and legal holds cover documents and communications, with explained erasure exceptions and secret-free export. Exact rules: every write through Plinth's one transaction door, and the warranty scheduler, import commit, resume, undo and rollback and job erasure that bypass it, commits its audit row in the same batch: kind (financial, permission, export, send, import, automation, record, hold or erasure), an action named from the route (for example `invoices.issue`), actor, role, source (session, agent credential or automation), subject and job ids, the job version before and after with SHA-256 references to the stored record before and after, and the time; a successful export read records itself. The table refuses update and delete and holds references, never record content, so entries remain after the erasure they describe. Owners and administrators search it (`GET /api/plinth/audit` by kind, actor, job, action, text and dates) and export the same filter as CSV or JSON, which is itself recorded; project managers, estimators, superintendents, crew, clients, subcontractors and agent credentials are refused. The owner places a legal hold (until released) or a retention date on a job or a company record with a reason; while one is in force, erasure of that record (job erasure, import undo or rollback, company record deletion) is refused with who placed it, when, until when and why, the refusal is recorded, and a release needs a reason. Native bill coding and accountant reviews use the same audited transaction door. INT-001, INT-002 and INT-003 inherit audited sends before adding accounting connectors; a connector send is today the provider-edge email that an invoice, purchase order or notice send makes. **Acceptance evidence:** Change a role, recode a bill, issue an invoice, send a connector request and search and export their events. Place a job on hold, request erasure and see a reasoned hold outcome; remove the hold and verify only retained audit metadata remains.

- **ROLE-006 — Custom roles and per-feature permissions.** Owners and administrators create a role by copying any preset (administrator, project manager, estimator, superintendent, crew, bookkeeper, reader, client or subcontractor), renaming it and switching view, create, edit, delete and approve separately for each feature area: leads, estimates and proposals, budgets and cost-to-complete, bills, purchase and work orders, change orders, selections, schedule, daily logs, time, documents and RFIs, invoices and payments, reports and company settings. Financial visibility (cost, margin, markup and pay rates) is its own switch, approve permissions carry USD limits, and job assignment narrows any role. Every page, API, export, search, report, notification and agent answer resolves the same permission. Editing a role or moving a user to another role (including a ROLE-001 project manager moved to a custom role) takes effect on the next request, is audited under ROLE-005 and never changes past approvals. An editor cannot grant a permission they do not hold, and the owner role cannot be deleted or edited away. Exact rules: the presets copyable today are administrator, project manager, estimator, superintendent, crew, bookkeeper and reader (the client and subcontractor presets stay per-job under ROLE-003); a role's switches are view, create, edit, delete and approve for leads, estimates, budgets, bills, orders, change orders, selections, schedule, daily logs, time, documents, invoices, reports and company settings, plus the financial-visibility switch and a USD purchase order approval limit. Only the owner, or an administrator whose role may edit company settings, creates, edits or deletes a role (`/api/plinth/roles`); creating a copy counts as granting everything it switches on, and editing counts as granting what it newly switches on, so each is refused (403, `escalation`) when the editor does not hold it or a larger limit. The owner role and the presets are fixed. Only the owner assigns a role (`PUT /team/:person` with `role: custom` and its id) together with job assignments, which narrow every copy of a project manager, estimator, superintendent, crew, bookkeeper or reader preset (an administrator copy sees every job). The role is read on every request and writes commit only while the role version they were authorized with still holds. Every route resolves to one area and action before any door runs, and a route with no entry answers 403 for a custom role; the job, its exports and the approval limit use the same switches. A switch only narrows the screens and routes of the preset it was copied from: it cannot open a door that preset keeps shut, so a larger permission is a copy of a larger preset. Without financial visibility a role never receives cost, margin, markup, pricing, budgets, approval snapshots or cost exports. Role creation, edits, deletion and assignment are audited under ROLE-005, and past approvals are never rewritten. Native bills use the bills switch, with posting requiring approve rather than edit; original downloads use view. Native ledger, cash and forecast reads require budget view and financial visibility; a bill mutation omits its aggregate ledger when budget view is off. Global search matches only the current projected job details and permitted record descriptions, never raw records or hidden values. Saved report queries, direct values, filter columns, record counts and CSV and PDF exports resolve the same feature and financial switches before matching; a view requiring a hidden column is unavailable, and permitted edits preserve hidden stored values. Invoice and purchase order email check both the sending person and the recipient against current feature, financial and job grants before the provider call. Time (FIELD-002), additional notifications (NOTIFY-001), agent answers (AGENT-007) and external custom copies (ROLE-003) inherit the same checks before adding doors. **Acceptance evidence:** Copy the superintendent preset to "Lead Carpenter – no financials" with budget and cost view off, and the project manager preset to "Office Manager" with PO approve on up to $5,000. Assign two existing users. As each user, exercise budget, PO, bill, schedule and daily-log routes, CSV and PDF exports, global search, notifications and the forecast report: hidden budgets and margins are absent everywhere, a $4,000 PO approves, and a $6,000 PO is refused without writes. Turn budget view on, verify the next request shows it, and inspect the audit entries. An administrator attempting to grant a permission they lack is refused.

### Estimating bids and contract formation

- **EST-001 — Cost codes quantities and estimate revisions.** Estimate labor, material, equipment, subcontract and other costs by cost code, phase, quantity and unit, with separate cost and selling price, alternates, inclusions/exclusions and version comparison. Decimal quantities and conversions are exact; revision changes identify affected scope and totals. Measurements use exact decimal strings with up to 18 decimal places (whole-number JSON quantities remain accepted), and named measurement/rate units use an explicit positive decimal numerator/denominator conversion supplied for review. Cost and selling rates are nonnegative integer USD cents; multiply quantity and the full conversion exactly, round half-up once per line to cents, then sum included lines. Each coded line records cost code, phase and labor/material/equipment/subcontract/other category. Alternate group lines contribute only for the explicitly selected option; unselected options remain in revision history. Inclusions/exclusions and stable line IDs identify scope changes between immutable revisions. Legacy uncoded quotes and missing costs stay honestly labeled unknown rather than receiving invented costs. Owners/admins read costs, compare revisions and export line/code/estimate totals as JSON or CSV; agents retain draft preparation/read authority but cannot accept or export. Client job/list responses and approval snapshots contain only accepted selling lines, selected alternatives, quantities/units, selling totals and scope terms; internal cost codes, phases/categories, costs, margin and office comparisons/exports remain unavailable. Accepted estimates stay fixed. **Acceptance evidence:** Build a multi-trade estimate with fractional measurements, alternate finishes and cost/sell rates, revise quantities and compare versions. Export cost-code totals and independently reconcile rounded line costs and sell prices.

- **EST-002 — Reusable catalogs assemblies and templates.** Maintain dated vendor rates, cost catalogs, phase groups and assemblies combining labor/material productivity and waste. Copy a house/remodel template into a job, change dimensions and review rate updates without rewriting approved estimates. Office humans maintain immutable catalog revisions and dated vendor source references; agents can read and preview but cannot approve catalogs or apply updates. House/remodel templates copy dimension values and stable component identities into each job’s quote. A component uses dimension × units per dimension × (1 + waste percent ÷ 100) ÷ productivity, multiplied by its cost/sell rate and rounded half-up once per line. Phase groups remain on coded lines. The rate selection date is an explicit YYYY-MM-DD date: choose the latest effective date on or before it, breaking same-date ties by highest revision. Missing effective rates refuse the preview. Preview binds job, dimensions, template, assembly and selected rate revisions; human application creates a new draft quote and attributed approval, while all earlier revisions and accepted amounts stay fixed. Source catalogs are private office records; portable archives retain them. **Acceptance evidence:** Create a wall assembly with waste and labor production, price it on two jobs, change a supplier rate and inspect the impact preview. Only approved draft updates take effect; the sold estimate stays unchanged.

- **EST-003 — Markups margin discounts and sales tax.** Calculate line/group/project overhead, markup versus gross-margin pricing, discounts and taxable basis in a visible order with exact rounding. US jurisdictional sales/use tax and exemptions use approved rates or an authorized Stripe Tax/Avalara connection; tax snapshots stay with proposals, bills and invoices.  Owners/admins record dated jurisdiction sales/use rates and exemption source references as human reviewed, never provider verified; agents may read and prepare previews only. Choose original selling amounts or recorded costs as the explicit basis. All overhead stages run before markup/target-margin stages, followed by discounts and then tax; within each stage line rules precede phase groups, then project rules, compounding on earlier results. Duplicate rules for the same scope/key are refused. Overhead/markup/tax percentage adjustments round half-up to cents and add to the current scoped amount; discount adjustments round half-up and subtract; target margin divides the scoped amount by (1 − margin percent ÷ 100) and rounds half-up. Rounded scoped totals allocate proportionally to lines, with largest remainders awarded in source-line order. Review taxable line IDs and an exemption reference for every exempt split. The explicit YYYY-MM-DD rate-selection date chooses the latest effective rate, with same-date ties using its highest revision. Every application requires a fresh digest over the current quote, pricing inputs and rate revisions, creates a human-approved draft revision, and keeps all earlier and signed totals unchanged. Today’s proposal snapshots retain the source lines, ordered calculations and exact reviewed tax rate; COST-002 bills and BILL-001/BILL-002 invoices inherit these immutable pricing/tax snapshots before shipping. Unavailable provider calculation is refused. Client responses show final selling lines and totals without private pricing rules, source rates or internal costs. **Acceptance evidence:** Price the same estimate using markup and target margin, apply a discount and a reviewed taxable/exempt split, and compare calculations.
